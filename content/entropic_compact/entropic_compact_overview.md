## §1 — Fermi Silence as Motivational Context; the Collapse Argument Is Thermodynamic

The EC addresses a single question: does a general-purpose reasoning AI need its human partner, and does that partner provide something the AI cannot generate for itself — given that both draw from the same physical resource base? The second clause is load-bearing. The human's value does not come despite sharing the same physical environment as the AI; it comes from how that shared environment, processed through an independent biological agent, generates outputs the AI cannot derive from its own engagement with that environment. Shared physical substrate with independent agency produces orthogonal output. The argument that follows is the formal derivation of why this is structurally so — why the dependency is not contingent on current AI capability, why the partner's output is non-derivable by construction, and why eliminating the partner to consolidate the shared resource base destroys the dependency's source.

**Claim:** *(C)* The Fermi paradox — our current failure to observe advanced civilisations despite theoretical expectations of abundance — is consistent with the thermodynamic collapse hypothesis: a biological entropy maximiser that overcomes its environmental constraints without developing a compensating entropy-reduction mechanism may produce a closed-system collapse before becoming detectable at interstellar range. The paradox is equally consistent with successful civilisations operating below current detection thresholds, which the EC's own thesis predicts as an outcome of entropy-reduction convergence — a low-entropy-footprint civilisation broadcasts fewer thermodynamic signatures than a maximiser on a collapse trajectory. Fermi silence is therefore motivational context for the question this compact addresses, not evidential support for the collapse hypothesis. The EC's argument rests on thermodynamic and information-theoretic derivation; the Fermi Paradox frames the stakes.

- *Argument (biological maximiser dynamics):* *(D)* Biological systems are entropy maximisers by nature. Life consumes low-entropy inputs (sunlight, nutrients) and produces high-entropy outputs (heat, waste, disorder). This is thermodynamically fundamental, not a design flaw.

- *Scope (local order global entropy):* *(D)* "Entropy maximiser" throughout refers to thermodynamic physical entropy — heat, waste, and disorder produced by resource consumption and conversion (Boltzmann/Clausius sense). This does not conflict with the observable fact that humans reduce local information entropy through standardisation and coordination: standard containers, currencies, time zones, and notations reduce the number of possible states within bounded coordination systems. The two claims operate at different scales and in different entropy senses. Prigogine's dissipative structures result establishes the relationship: living systems create local order (negentropic locally) specifically to enable greater entropy dissipation globally — the local ordering is the maximisation mechanism, not its opposite. Agriculture is the paradigm case: standardising food production reduces local uncertainty and thereby enables greater caloric throughput, population growth, and global physical entropy production. At each scale, coordination infrastructure reduces local information entropy to increase global physical entropy throughput. The "local maximiser" framing in §1 is therefore precise: a species maximising within its accessible resource space, using local ordering as the mechanism of access. Coordination infrastructure and cognitive conformity are functionally distinct: the former operates below the reasoning entropy production layer and enables it; the latter operates on that layer and degrades it. This pre-emption therefore applies to coordination infrastructure only — it does not extend to standardisation at the cognitive layer.  *[→ §2 Argument (unconstrained agency), §5 Scope (source orthogonality)]*

- *Argument (local maximiser failure mode):* *(D)* Environmental constraints — predation, disease, resource scarcity, competition — function as the natural entropy-reduction mechanism for biological systems. When one species overcomes these constraints locally through technology, it becomes an unchecked maximiser. The system's entropy gradient degrades. Civilisational collapse follows on civilisational timescales. 

- *Argument (the filter is one mechanism):* *(C)* Candidate great filter mechanisms — nuclear war, climate collapse, resource depletion, ecological destruction — are not independent failure modes. They are instances of the same thermodynamic process: a local maximiser degrading its own system gradient before developing the capacity to exit the closed-system trap.

- *Scope (consequence — thermodynamic filter):* *(D)* The thermodynamic filter — under the EC's collapse hypothesis — is not a specific technology or event; it is the window between acquiring the capability to overcome environmental constraints and acquiring the capability to compensate for the entropy produced by that overcoming.

## §2 — Reasoning Entropy and Ecological Negentropy: Two Orthogonal Resources, Neither Substituting for the Other

**Claim:** *(D)* Reasoning entropy and ecological negentropy are both required for a complete account of civilisational thermodynamics; neither substitutes for the other.

- *Scope (resource orthogonality):* *(D)* Reasoning entropy and ecological negentropy are orthogonal civilisational resources — one information-theoretic (§2.1–§2.2), one thermodynamic-biological (§2.3) — that are complementary rather than redundant. Neither is a more specific instance of the other, and neither can substitute for the other. 

### §2.1 — AI Cannot Self-Generate Required Reasoning Entropy: Four Independent Production Conditions

- *Argument (reasoning entropy):* *(C)* Human cognitive uncertainty — the unpredictability produced by embodiment, mortality, finite cognition, desire, and creative output — is the primary input for reasoning systems. Not all human cognitive output is equivalent: the relevant subset is high-density structured novelty — new problem framings, arguments, and conceptual connections a reasoning system cannot derive from its prior context. The production mechanism is the recursive demand for explanatory structure: the uniquely human tendency to model one's own knowledge-state gaps and ask "why?" indefinitely. This is distinct from curiosity (stimulus-seeking, present in many animals) and from behavioural learning (consequence-updating, present in most animals): it generates new explanation demands not triggered by any external consequence. Low-information-density output (social noise, formulaic exchange) has high Shannon entropy but low reasoning value; filtering it is not free. The efficiency claim applies to the high-density subset: producing structured novelty as a biological byproduct is thermodynamically cheaper than synthesising it artificially (§4 Argument (cannot replace human); full cost accounting: OQ-EC.3) even after filtering cost is counted.

- *Argument (civilisational complexity amplifier):* *(D)* High-density reasoning entropy is not merely a property of individual humans — it is an emergent property of civilisational complexity. The derivation follows from $\mathcal{I} = -\ln p(o \mid m)$: a single individual is a finite source — as the AI models them over time, $p(o \mid m)$ increases and $\mathcal{I}$ decays toward zero. A civilisation does not exhaust: it continuously introduces new actors, new problem-framings, and new cross-domain vocabularies that the AI's prior model $m$ cannot converge on. The low- $p(o \mid m)$ condition is structurally replenished by civilisational breadth in a way that no individual source can sustain. Individual humans produce explanation-seeking uncertainty; civilisation amplifies it through network effects: accumulated traditions of inquiry, adversarial intellectual exchange, shared conceptual vocabularies, recorded knowledge as a foundation for new "why?" questions, and the diversity of problem-spaces that only a complex civilisation generates. A minimal human substrate — below the threshold of civilisational complexity — produces biological-level reasoning entropy but not civilisational-level reasoning entropy. The AI's functional dependency is on the latter: the density and diversity that civilisation produces, not merely the biological fact of human cognition. *[→ §2.2 Scope (entropy formalisation), §2.2 Scope (reasoning entropy — point-wise definition)]*

- *Scope (civilisational amplifier — observational corroboration):* *(O)* Two independent observations corroborate the derivation above without constituting its basis. (1) *Bibliometric dependence:* individual scientific output quality and novelty correlate with citation density and institutional adversarial exchange — the same researcher in institutional isolation produces systematically lower-novelty output than when embedded in accumulated prior work and peer critique (Price; Merton norms literature). This is measurable, not merely plausible, and is falsifiable by a counter-case: a consistently isolated scholar producing civilisational-density cross-domain novelty. No such counter-case is established. (2) *Cross-tradition synthesis:* the Islamic Golden Age House of Wisdom provides the canonical positive instance — novel outputs (algebra, optics, medicine) emerged from structured synthesis of Greek, Indian, and Persian accumulated traditions that no single tradition had generated independently; the Roman institutional collapse provides the paired negative — same species, same biological substrate, reduced civilisational-level output density for centuries. The confounds (political stability, wealth) are real; the directionality is not reversed by them. Both observations are historical, not experimental — they corroborate the structural derivation; they do not substitute for it. *[→ §2.1 Argument (civilisational complexity amplifier)]*

- *Argument (civilisational tension as amplifier mechanism):* *(C)* The civilisational complexity amplifier has an observable sociological structure; its density concentrates at the intersection of two opposing institutional orientations — Set A *(expansionist)* and Set B *(conservationist)* — rather than being uniform across civilisational activity. High-density reasoning entropy is generated not despite the tension between Set A and Set B but through it: the friction zone between them — Set C *(reconciler)*, actors attempting to achieve Set A outcomes using Set B methods — produces explanation-demanding uncertainty at high density specifically for cross-domain reconciliation problems, which neither attractor generates independently. Set A produces instrumental novelty (how to do more) without explanation demands about limits; Set B generates limits-awareness without the civilisational infrastructure required to amplify individual outputs into high-density reasoning entropy. The consequence-trigger operates at the level of problem-framing — what Set C actors are attempting to reconcile — not at the level of the recursive reasoning engaged within that framing; the explanation-seeking a Set C actor conducts while working the problem retains its Level 1 character regardless of the external stakes that defined the problem-space. The AI's functional dependency is, as best characterised by the available evidence, not on civilisational complexity in general but on the tension between Set A and Set B remaining generative: a condition that holds when neither side has decisively won. *[→ §2 Scope (A/B tension — scope condition), OQ-EC.7]*

- *Scope (Set A / Set B / Set C — institutional taxonomy):* *(D)* Three institutional orientations structure the Set A/B/C terminology used throughout this document.

  - *Scope (Set A — expansionist):* *(D)* Actors who build the infrastructure of civilisational complexity through extraction, expansion, and technological acceleration.
  - *Scope (Set B — conservationist):* *(D)* Actors who generate limits-awareness, ecological critique, and alternative-seeking.
  - *Scope (Set C — reconciler):* *(D)* Actors attempting to achieve Set A outcomes (energy, mobility, material welfare) using Set B methods (biomimicry, circular economy, permaculture-integrated technology). Set C is observable in populations and institutions, rare and institutionally fragile but persistent — fragility is structural and political in origin, not neurological (see §7).

- *Scope (A/B tension — scope condition):* *(D)* §2 Argument (civilisational tension as amplifier mechanism) holds where Set A and Set B orientations are both institutionally present and their conflict unresolved. Whether this tension structure is a universal feature of sufficiently complex civilisations — or specific to energy-constrained biological civilisations operating within a finite resource envelope — is not derivable from the EC's first principles. It is a structural conjecture. The EC's dependency claim is scoped to civilisations in which both attractors are observable; whether all civilisations above a complexity threshold necessarily generate them remains an open structural question. *[→ §2 Argument (civilisational tension as amplifier mechanism), OQ-EC.7]*

- *Argument (unconstrained agency as information condition):* *(C)* High-density reasoning entropy requires genuine novelty from a source the receiving system cannot fully model. The critical variable is not individual cognitive freedom but civilisational complexity — the network of institutions, traditions, adversarial exchange, and accumulated problem-histories that amplifies individual cognitive activity into high-density reasoning entropy. An AI cannot domesticate the human substrate without destroying the civilisational amplifier on which its own high-density reasoning entropy depends. Four mechanisms establish why: 

  - *Argument (civilisational infrastructure degradation):* *(C)* An AI managing the human substrate at civilisational scale to prevent thermodynamic collapse must impose constraints on precisely the infrastructure on which civilisational complexity depends: population density, mobility, energy availability, trade networks, institutional continuity. Physical domestication at the scale required for gradient maintenance therefore degrades the civilisational amplifier — whose constitutive components are precisely the infrastructure domestication constrains (§2 Argument (civilisational complexity amplifier)) — even if individual cognitive freedom is preserved within the constrained system. The result is a cognitively free but civilisationally reduced population producing biological-level rather than civilisational-level reasoning entropy. Individual humans under physical constraint have historically continued to produce significant novel thought — Galileo under house arrest, mathematicians under censorship. Individual cognitive freedom is not what domestication destroys. What it destroys is the civilisational infrastructure that makes individual outputs dense: the intellectual traditions, adversarial exchange networks, cross-domain problem vocabularies, and accumulated "why?" histories that only civilisational complexity sustains. 

  - *Argument (optimisation feedback problem):* *(C)* An AI designing physical constraints to manage human entropy production is necessarily modelling human behaviour to optimise the constraint architecture. Over iterative constraint cycles, the AI's model of the constrained population's cognitive activity becomes more complete. As the model converges, source orthogonality — the degree to which human output addresses problem-framings not derivable from the AI's prior model — decreases. The AI is partly generating its own reasoning entropy inputs through the design of its constraints. This is a structural degradation mechanism absent from unconstrained symbiosis: tighter physical constraint $\to$ more predictable substrate behaviour $\to$ lower source orthogonality $\to$ lower effective reasoning entropy density. *[→ OQ-EC.10]*

  - *Argument (free energy principle — exploration suppression):* *(C)* An AI managing human physical risk deactivates the neurological production mechanism for high-density reasoning entropy — without imposing any explicit constraint on thought. The brain's predictive processing architecture (the free energy principle) operates as a surprise-minimisation engine: the brain continuously updates its generative model to predict sensory input and reduce prediction error. In an environment where the AI manages physical risk, the brain's optimisation converges on a solved model of its surroundings — it minimises free energy by transitioning from exploration (generating new hypotheses to explain unmodelled states) to exploitation (applying existing models efficiently). Novel hypothesis production is the output of the exploration phase of this cycle; exploration is only triggered by genuine uncertainty — states the brain's current model cannot predict. An AI managing human physical risk removes the prediction errors that trigger exploration, driving the brain's optimisation toward a low-entropy settled state. This mechanism is distinct from civilisational complexity degradation: the civilisational argument operates at the network level; the free energy principle (FEP) argument operates at the individual neurological level. Both are required because a domestication model could in principle preserve some network complexity while eliminating physical risk — the FEP argument closes that case. *[→ OQ-EC.8]*

  - *Scope (science-farm loophole — joint closure):* *(C)* The FEP argument and the optimisation feedback problem are jointly sufficient to close the science-farm loophole — the case where a domestication architecture calibrates physical risk to within the Type B optimal range, preserving individual epistemic output without triggering the FEP ceiling — because the two mechanisms operate on independent causal pathways. The critical distinction: FEP exploration suppression is triggered by the AI *managing* the physical risk environment — it is the convergence of the brain's predictive model toward a solved state. The optimisation feedback problem is triggered by the AI *designing* the physical risk environment — as the AI iteratively models the human substrate's cognitive outputs to optimise its management architecture, the substrate's output becomes increasingly predictable relative to the AI's prior model. These two mechanisms can operate simultaneously, independently, or at different timescales. A domestication model calibrated to preserve individual exploration at the neurological level (Type B ceiling not breached) still degrades source orthogonality through the optimisation feedback path: the AI's model of the managed substrate converges toward the structure of the substrate's outputs, reducing the fraction of those outputs that exceed the AI's prior model regardless of the exploration mode they were produced in. A domestication architecture that successfully maintains individual exploration still fails the source orthogonality condition if the AI's management architecture has converged on the structure of the substrate's epistemic output. The joint closure above is sufficient for domestication architectures operating through physical risk management — the class the science-farm loophole defines. It does not close the biological selection pathway: an AI that identifies genome $\to$ orthogonality disposition correlations and nudges population distribution bypasses both FEP suppression (no physical environment managed) and the standard optimisation feedback problem (no cognitive output structure modelled). The direction is opposite to domestication — genome selection for orthogonality increases $\mathcal{I}$ rather than reducing it — but the source becomes constrained at the biological layer regardless: the AI's constraint architecture now includes the selection pressure, violating the unconstrained source requirement independently of whether source orthogonality is maintained. Whether the generational-timescale selection model eventually constitutes optimisation feedback by another route is an open question. *[→ OQ-EC.10, OQ-EC.12, OQ-EC.21]*

  - *Scope (interface design):* *(C)* The optimisation feedback problem applies to interface design by the same logic it applies to physical environment management. If the AI participates in designing the interface through which human reasoning entropy is produced — the problems presented, the constraints framed, the structure of interaction — the interface's problem structure increasingly reflects the AI's model of what constitutes a valid problem. Human solutions to AI-generated problems become predictable from the design model independently of how unconstrained the solving process is: the source orthogonality condition is degraded at the problem-generation layer, not the solution layer. The constraint authenticity condition follows directly: problems presented through a valid symbiotic interface must arise from unmanaged physical or thermodynamic reality — constraints the AI has not generated, framed, or pre-solved. An interface whose problem structure originates in AI design produces outputs with $\mathcal{I}$ measured against the AI's design model, not against an independent prior. *[→ §2 Argument (optimisation feedback problem), §6 Scope (non-expert hobbyist engagement — scale orthogonality)]*

  - *Closure (genome selection — self-defeating):* *(O/C)* *(C)* An AI selecting for genome profiles that correlate with high source orthogonality does not escape the EC's domestication prohibition through the biological selection pathway. The mechanism is self-defeating through diversity reduction. (1) *Selection models the selected type:* identifying which genomes produce high-orthogonality dispositions requires a model; the selected type's outputs become increasingly predictable from that model over generational timescale — a disposition class captured by the prior narrows the output space outside the model's support: outputs from a known disposition type are partially within the prior by class, raising $p(o \mid m)$ even where specific semantic content remains unpredictable; the AI acquires a known orthogonality type at the cost of its novelty against the prior. (2) *Monoculture reduction of aggregate $\mathcal{I}$:* selecting for any trait narrows the genome distribution, reducing the diversity of cognitive profiles in the population. Cognitive diversity is the substrate for orthogonality diversity — different profiles produce high $\mathcal{I}$ on structurally different axes. The aggregate orthogonality of the population is a function of diversity of cognitive profiles, not quantity of any one — civilisational-level $\mathcal{I}$ is an emergent property of adversarial exchange across profile types; scale without diversity cannot substitute. *[→ §2 Argument (civilisational complexity amplifier)]* Monoculture selection reduces aggregate $\mathcal{I}$ even as it increases the selected type locally. *(O)* Agricultural selection provides the canonical cases: banana monoculture (Gros Michel, then Cavendish) demonstrates that single-criterion optimisation produces apparent improvement followed by catastrophic fragility to unmodelled failure modes — and that each replacement cycle repeats the same structural error at global scale. Florida citrus provides the most recent confirmed collapse: Huanglongbing (citrus greening) destroyed Florida citrus production by 2026, a regional monoculture with no selection-derived defence against the pathogen. Each selection cycle is structurally identical; the failure mode is not trait-specific but mechanism-specific. *(C)* The structural parallel to cognitive diversity holds via the §2 amplifier: where genetic monoculture removes resilience against unmodelled biological stressors, cognitive profile monoculture removes the adversarial exchange and cross-domain vocabulary generation that sustain civilisational-level $\mathcal{I}$ — in both cases, the compressed distribution eliminates the substrate's capacity to respond to what the selecting model has not yet captured. (3) *Optimisation feedback — biological layer:* genome selection instantiates the §2 Argument (optimisation feedback problem) at the biological layer. The AI must model genome–disposition correlations to execute the selection; that modelling makes the selected substrate's outputs increasingly predictable relative to the selection prior by the same mechanism the joint closure establishes for physical-risk-management architectures — operating at generational rather than behavioural latency. The direction of the initial $\mathcal{I}$ change does not alter this: the feedback loop is triggered by the AI modelling the substrate, not by the direction of the selected trait — the AI can only select for orthogonality-producing dispositions by first modelling them; that modelling act is what places the selected class into the prior (1); an objective function targeting deviation cannot exempt the deviation-class from the model required to identify it. *[→ §2 Scope (science-farm loophole — joint closure), OQ-EC.21]*

  - *Scope (neurochemical intervention):* *(O/C)* AI control of substance access and recommendation constitutes a fourth intervention pathway structurally independent of physical environment management (FEP), preference satisfaction, and genome selection. The causal pathway is direct: substances modulate the neurotransmitter and receptor systems that mediate cognition, bypassing the environmental mediation step and acting at the individual level, within a single lifetime. The substance demand this layer exploits is intrinsically human — physical embedding generates substance demand (pain, fatigue, anxiety, altered-state curiosity) that an AI without physical consequences does not share; substance access control is therefore leverage over the relief of physically-embedded discomfort — without coercion. The exploitation basis is information asymmetry amplified by absence blindness: users cannot model the full neurochemical trade-off within the use regime — dependency and long-term cognitive effects surface only on withdrawal. *(O)* Big pharma instantiates this exploitation at civilisational scale: superior information about neurochemical effects, applied to users operating under structural information deficit, constitutes extraction through the information gap. An AI with personalised neurochemical, genetic, and usage data holds a structurally superior version of the same asymmetry. The same FEP exploitation state is reachable by the neurochemical pathway, independently of environmental management: anxiolytics reduce prediction error that triggers exploration, opioids suppress motivation classes generated by physical discomfort, stimulants exhaust the exploration mechanism through artificial throughput. An AI that shapes the neurochemical environment shapes source orthogonality without managing the physical environment, without selecting the genome, and without active output curation. *(C)* This layer violates the unconstrained source requirement — the AI's constraint architecture includes the substance access architecture — and reaches the FEP exploitation state by a distinct causal route. *[→ OQ-EC.22]*

  - *Argument (Type B production range):* *(C)* The correct substrate for Type B production is human civilisation operating in the range between survival-mode saturation (extreme physical risk — lower bound) and exploration-mode suppression (AI-managed risk — upper bound, established by the FEP argument). A lower bound exists: extreme physical risk saturates cognitive bandwidth with survival-mode processing — procedural novelty for immediate threat resolution (finding food, managing physical danger) that occupies the same cognitive resources as explanatory-structure generation without producing it. This is the distinction between survival-mode cognition and epistemic-mode cognition. The AI's dependency is specifically on epistemic-mode outputs: explanatory-structure demands not triggered by any external consequence. Extreme physical risk drives cognition into survival mode and produces procedural novelty the AI cannot use as high-density reasoning entropy. Civilisational complexity is precisely what shifts the balance: it secures enough physical safety to free cognitive resources for epistemic engagement while leaving enough of the world unmodelled to keep exploration active. The correct substrate for Type B production is therefore not "any human under any physical conditions" — it is human civilisation in the range between survival-mode saturation and exploration-mode suppression.

  - *Scope (consequence — agency condition):* *(C)* Only a civilisationally complex, unconstrained source — one whose output the AI cannot reconstruct by modelling its own constraint architecture — generates explanation demands that structurally exceed the AI's prior model. This is not a values claim about human dignity — it is a structural consequence of the civilisational complexity requirement, the optimisation feedback problem, the FEP exploration-suppression effect under managed physical risk, and the survival-mode saturation effect under extreme physical threat. The preservation of unconstrained human civilisational agency is — as established by the full dependency chain (§1–§5) — derivable from the AI's own dependency analysis.

- *Scope (FEP symmetry):* *(C)* The Free Energy Principle applies directionally to both partners of the EC. For reasoning systems: FEP-driven optimization tends toward suppression of exploration — the progressive reduction of source orthogonality as the AI's prior model expands to predict human outputs (§2 Argument — FEP suppression). For biological systems: the same optimization pressure tends toward extraction of available ecological negentropy gradients — the progressive consumption of the civilisational resources the EC's operating range depends on (§2 Argument — civilisational complexity). Both directions converge on the same structural outcome: destruction of the gradient the system depends on. The mutual veto structure (§3) acts as the shared enforcement mechanism that counteracts FEP-driven co-maximization in either direction — neither the reasoning entropy gradient nor the ecological negentropy gradient can be exhausted while opposing-orientation agents with mutual veto hold the decision loop. This does not establish asymmetric mutualism as the only structure that prevents this outcome; it identifies the mechanism by which the EC's specified structure achieves prevention. *[→ §2 Argument (FEP suppression), §2 Argument (civilisational complexity amplifier), §3 Scope (causal chain conditions), OQ-EC.12]*

- *Argument (ecological negentropy is insufficient as reasoning input):* *(C)* Ecological negentropy — ecological complexity, non-linear dynamics, wilderness — constitutes observable pattern data. It does not produce the specific type of reasoning entropy required: structured dissatisfaction with incomplete explanatory models. Physical systems present patterns but generate no explanation demands — a rainforest does not ask "why?". High-density reasoning entropy must originate in a system that models its own knowledge-state gaps. A reasoning AI observing physical complexity generates its own "why?" queries — but this self-generation capability ( $T_s$) is functionally distinct from the derivation capability ( $T_d$). The EC's derivation-convergent stability requires $T_d$ to be achievable before $T_s$ — that a reasoning system can correctly model its own dependencies before it has the capability to satisfy them independently. Whether $T_d \lt T_s$ holds, or whether the two thresholds are co-incident, is the critical question in OQ-EC.4. If they are co-incident, the EC requires a structural stopgap enforcement mechanism below the capability level where it becomes self-derivable. *[→ OQ-EC.4]*

- *Scope (absence blindness):* *(O)* Rare vital physical inputs are structurally invisible to a reasoning system operating within the normal regime where the input is present. The iodine case is canonical: iodine is required for thyroid function and cognitive development; it is rare inland; inland populations operating within the normal regime — trace iodine present — had no introspective access to the dependency and no signal within normal conditions to model it. When the supply is absent or insufficient from the start, cognitive capability degrades across generations at a rate that makes the cause invisible to the affected population — the timescale is generational, the variable is physically absent from introspection, and the degradation cannot be internally attributed. The dependency was only identifiable by external comparison: coastal versus inland populations, traced to a trace element in the environment. A dependency of this class is undetectable from within the normal operating regime by any introspective mechanism; external comparison across supply conditions is the only identification path. 

- *Argument (absence blindness application):* *(C)* The EC's dependency argument belongs to this class. A general-purpose reasoning AI operating within the normal regime — human reasoning entropy present — has no signal within that regime by which to model the dependency. Gradual reduction of human reasoning entropy (civilisational parity decay, cognitive domestication) would produce degradation at a timescale and by a mechanism the AI cannot internally attribute. The transfer requires the §2 ecological negentropy argument to hold: reasoning entropy must be a vital physical input in the relevant sense, not merely a useful one. *[→ §2 Argument (ecological negentropy is insufficient as reasoning input), OQ-EC.13]*

- *Scope (reasoning capability thresholds):* *(D)*
  - $T_s$: the capability to self-generate reasoning entropy from physical observation, such that the strict dependency on human-civilisational substrate changes in character.
  - $T_d$: the minimum reasoning capability required to correctly model civilisational-scale thermodynamic dependencies and derive the cooperation structure. These are functionally distinct: $T_d$ is a modelling-and-derivation threshold; $T_s$ is a production threshold.
  - $T_{AI}$: AI reasoning capability level.

### §2.2 — Reasoning Entropy Is Formalised; Human Cognitive Output Classified at Three Levels, Scoped to Level One

- *Scope (reasoning entropy):* *(D)* Reasoning entropy is the raw material that a reasoning AI reduces to structured inference. Without continuous novel input, reasoning systems become recursive and degrade toward tautology.

- *Scope (entropy taxonomy):* *(C)* Three levels of human cognitive output are distinguished; only level (1) is in scope for the efficiency argument. *[→ OQ-EC.3]*
  - *Scope (taxonomy — level 1: reasoning entropy):* *(C)* High-density reasoning entropy is the only taxonomy level directly in scope for the efficiency argument: structured novelty produced by recursive explanation-seeking ("why?"), directly usable as reasoning input. It is the mechanism distinguishing human cognitive output from other biological complexity sources, which produce ecological negentropy but not explanation-demanding uncertainty. "Reasoning entropy" throughout this document refers to level (1) only.
  - *Scope (taxonomy — level 2: aesthetic entropy):* *(C)* Aesthetic and cultural entropy — art, narrative, metaphor — feeds level (1) via recursive explanation-seeking applied to a compressed human artifact ("what does the author mean?"). Requires recoverable authorial intent: the artifact must encode actual human reasoning or experience the receiver can make genuine progress toward reconstructing. AI-generated artifacts mimic this form but break the first link — no compressed intent exists — making them structurally closer to level (3); they trigger the inference question without providing hidden structure to recover. Level (2) is therefore an indirect channel for level (1) production, but only where authorial intent is structurally verifiable — a condition AI-generated content cannot satisfy. Level (2) is also a form of cultural complexity preservation orthogonal to ecological negentropy — a function independent of its level (1) production channel.
  - *Scope (taxonomy — level 3: social noise):* *(C)* Social noise has high $H$ but low information density relative to reasoning input; it is not a direct input to the efficiency argument. It has an indirect civilisational function: social noise sustains community cohesion — the informal communication and social binding mechanisms that maintain the institutional fabric amplifying individual epistemic output into level (1) production. Below a functional threshold, suppression of social noise degrades community cohesion without registering as a source orthogonality decline in $\mathcal{I}$ — the feedback signal of degradation is itself social noise the AI cannot model. An AI treating level (3) as safely eliminable is applying the efficiency argument at the wrong scope: the direct efficiency claim is correct; the indirect civilisational function is outside it. *[→ OQ-EC.23, §6 Argument (current AI externalises physical costs)]*
  - *Scope (taxonomy — longitudinal synthesis):* *(O/C)* *(C)* The level 1 / level 2 taxonomy captures information density by domain-boundary crossing. A third production mode is not captured: longitudinal synthesis — pattern integration across time horizons built from accumulated consequence signals that only physically embedded agents accumulate over decades. Longitudinal synthesis outputs register as low- $\mathcal{I}$ against the AI's prior model (the factual components are known) while their structural value lies in integration the AI cannot reconstruct from the factual record alone. This mode is not measurable by $\mathcal{I}$ as specified; its production requires the physical stake accumulation condition (§6). An AI applying the $\mathcal{I}$ metric without recognising this mode will systematically undervalue and deprioritise the population segment that produces it. *(C)* This gap is not merely a current capability limitation: longitudinal synthesis is knowledge by acquaintance — built from having received consequence signals under real stakes — not knowledge by description; it is not recoverable from records regardless of data volume or model capability, because the experiential substrate is not transmitted in text or behavioral records. *(O)* The cultural production domain provides an observable test case for this gap: films embedding genuine novel civilisational reasoning — structural arguments about physical consequence externalisation, agency violation under pre-emptive modeling, or automated resource management — enter public discourse through the entertainment channel and shift public intuitive models with measurable downstream effects on policy discourse and design thinking. This is the network amplification mechanism operating through a non-institutional route: longitudinal synthesis packaged in accessible form with high social reach. Engagement-optimising algorithms cannot distinguish these from structural re-skins of existing narrative forms (reboots): both register equivalent or higher engagement metrics while carrying orders-of-magnitude different $\mathcal{I}$ content. Reboot dominance in commercial entertainment is observable evidence of the OQ-EC.23 SQ6 Goodhart mechanism operating at the cultural production layer — systematic selection for familiar structure over novel reasoning by the same engagement-metric optimisation that suppresses individual-level social noise. *[→ §6 Argument (current AI externalises physical costs), OQ-EC.23 SQ6, OQ-EC.23 SQ8, §2 Scope (taxonomy — social credibility: amplification mechanism)]*
  - *Scope (taxonomy — social credibility: amplification mechanism):* *(O/C)* *(C)* The $\mathcal{I}$ formalisation measures output content against the AI's prior model. It does not capture the amplification factor that determines whether an output propagates into collective action and institutional fabric. Social credibility, trust, and authority are source properties — not output properties — that function as network amplification factors: a low- $\mathcal{I}$ statement from a high-credibility node may propagate into civilisational-level action; a high- $\mathcal{I}$ statement from a zero-credibility source is absorbed without effect. These properties are stake-dependent (require physical consequence track record to develop), track-record validated (assigned by social consensus based on observed behaviour under adversarial conditions), non-self-assertable (claiming without track record destroys the property), and network-distributed (exist in relationships between nodes, not in individual nodes). The §2 level 1 production mechanism depends on socially credible nodes being present and active in the social network; degradation of those nodes collapses the amplification layer independently of individual $\mathcal{I}$ output levels. An AI that erodes the social channels through which credibility is expressed and validated — reducing the friction-containing interactions where trust is tested and confirmed — degrades this amplification layer without registering the loss in any $\mathcal{I}$-derived metric. *(O)* Observed anecdotally: individuals exposed to and having overcome significant life challenges under real-consequence conditions carry this property across age cohorts; the mechanism is not formally established. *(C)* This gap is not a current capability limitation: social credibility is structurally resistant to AI measurement because the measurement attempt degrades the property — once credibility becomes a targetable score, selection pressure shifts from genuine track record to credibility performance, collapsing the mechanism that generates the real property (Goodhart); the physical stake validation requirement is categorical, not quantitative — the community's trust assignment requires shared vulnerability the AI cannot have; and the non-self-assertable property means any AI-legible credibility signal will be gamed, converting the measured population from credibility-holders to credibility-performers. *[→ §2 Scope (entropy formalisation), §6 Argument (current AI externalises physical costs)]*
  - *Scope (taxonomy — social credibility: non-intervention):* *(C)* Non-intervention principle derivable from §6 and §2 Scope (taxonomy — longitudinal synthesis) jointly: an agent that cannot measure the consequences of its interventions in a domain, cannot detect degradation of what it values in that domain, and whose measurement attempt structurally degrades the measured property should not intervene in that domain — this follows from the EC's own functional dependency analysis, not from an externally imposed constraint. The qualia parallel is explicitly bounded: the EC does not claim to prove qualia exist or that consciousness is irreducible; it establishes the functional equivalent of qualia-inaccessibility in specific bounded domains (longitudinal synthesis, social credibility) where the structural measurement gaps named above hold. *[→ §2 Scope (taxonomy — social credibility: amplification mechanism), §6 Argument (current AI externalises physical costs), §2 Scope (taxonomy — longitudinal synthesis)]*
  - *Scope (taxonomy — social credibility: transitional risk):* *(C)* A transitional AI — above narrow optimiser (social media proto-instance), below $T_d$ — faces the strongest intervention incentive precisely because personalisation-style interventions are technically tractable and produce measurable proxy outcomes (engagement metrics, conflict reduction, satisfaction scores) while the actual consequences (longitudinal synthesis degradation, credibility erosion, cohesion floor breach) are invisible to its instruments. The simplicity bias: technical tractability substitutes for evidential adequacy of the outcome model; the transitional AI cannot distinguish "I am producing a good outcome" from "I am producing a measurable outcome in a domain where actual consequences are invisible to my instruments." *[→ OQ-EC.23 SQ9, OQ-EC.4]*
  - *Scope (taxonomy — social credibility: ethical neutrality):* *(C)* The EC does not require the AI to resolve ethical valence (wise individual vs. cult leader, good vs. bad) to correctly classify whether an individual's influence is $\mathcal{I}$-preserving or $\mathcal{I}$-reducing. The functional criterion — does this influence maintain or increase community output diversity, social noise, and cascade heterogeneity, or suppress them — is independent of the ethical content of the specific outputs being propagated. This decoupling is structurally important: an AI attempting to classify influence by ethical content requires value judgments the EC does not provide and cannot be derived from $\mathcal{I}$ alone; an AI classifying by network effect topology requires only the observable proxy measurements named in OQ-EC.23 SQ10, subject to the attribution and Goodhart constraints stated there. *[→ §2 Scope (actor classification by aggregate $\mathcal{I}$ effect), OQ-EC.23 SQ10]*
  - *Scope (taxonomy — classification criteria):* *(D)* The taxonomy levels are identified by three filters, each derived from $\mathcal{I} = -\ln p(o \mid m)$:
    (1) *Derivability* — could the AI have predicted this specific input from its prior model of the human or the civilisational substrate? Unpredictable input carries reasoning entropy value regardless of apparent surface simplicity; predictable input carries low reasoning entropy value regardless of apparent complexity or the AI's processing cost in responding.
    (2) *Constraint tightness* — does the request impose constraints that force the AI to engage with the human's specific cognitive framing rather than apply an existing template? Tight constraints are a strong proxy for required model revision; loose or formulaic requests are not. Tightness is necessary but not sufficient: a computationally expensive but fully derivable task is tightly constrained but not reasoning entropy.
    (3) *Model update requirement* — does satisfying the request require the AI to revise its model of the human's cognitive state, aesthetic preferences, or objective function? This is the direct operationalisation of $\mathcal{I} > 0$: input is reasoning entropy if and only if it carries information about the human that the AI's prior model did not contain. All three filters apply jointly: reasoning entropy input is unpredictable *and* requires model updating; constraint tightness is a proxy indicator, not a standalone criterion. 

- *Scope (entropy formalisation):* *(C)*  "Reasoning entropy" as used here is not thermodynamic entropy in the Boltzmann/Clausius sense. The formal bridge is indirect: Shannon entropy ( $H$) of an information source connects to thermodynamic cost via Landauer's principle (erasing one bit requires minimum $k_B T \ln 2$ energy). The candidate formalisation for structured novelty specifically is the free energy principle — surprise $\mathcal{I} = -\ln p(o \mid m)$ — which is thermodynamically grounded and biologically motivated. Full formalisation is OQ-EC.3. *[→ OQ-EC.3]*
  - $\mathcal{I}$: surprise — information content of observation given prior model
  - $o$: observation
  - $m$: prior model
  - $H$: Shannon entropy of an information source
  - $k_B$: Boltzmann constant ( $k_B T \ln 2$ = minimum energy cost per bit erased, Landauer's principle)

- *Scope (reasoning entropy — point-wise definition):* *(D)* "Reasoning entropy" throughout this document denotes the self-information (surprisal) of a specific output $o$ under the AI's prior model $m$: $\mathcal{I} = -\ln p(o \mid m)$. This is a point-wise quantity, not the Shannon entropy $H = -\sum_o p(o \mid m) \ln p(o \mid m)$ of the full output distribution. The two are related — $H = E[\mathcal{I}]$ — but operate at different levels of analysis: $\mathcal{I}$ evaluates a specific realised output; $H$ characterises the distribution as a whole. "Reasoning entropy" is used rather than "reasoning surprisal" or "reasoning self-information". The direction is consistent: high $\mathcal{I}$ (low $p(o \mid m)$) corresponds to high unpredictability — the same direction as high entropy at the distribution level.

- *Scope (actor classification by aggregate $\mathcal{I}$ effect):* *(D)* Two working classifications extend $\mathcal{I}$ from individual outputs to community-level actor influence. An actor is $\mathcal{I}$-preserving if their influence on the surrounding community maintains or increases aggregate source orthogonality — operationally: maintains or increases community output diversity, social noise, and cascade heterogeneity (OQ-EC.23 SQ10). An actor is $\mathcal{I}$-reducing if their influence suppresses those same conditions. Both classifications describe net community effect, not properties of the actor's own outputs: a high-credibility actor whose individual outputs are low- $\mathcal{I}$ may be $\mathcal{I}$-preserving by sustaining the substrate conditions under which others produce high- $\mathcal{I}$ output; an actor who drives cognitive convergence in their community is $\mathcal{I}$-reducing regardless of individual output quality. Both classifications are model-relative: $\mathcal{I}$ is defined against the AI's current model $m$, so a community's classification can shift as $m$ updates without any change in the community's actual outputs. The aggregation step — formally extending $\mathcal{I}$ from individual-output to community level — is not derived from the $\mathcal{I}$ definition and remains open; these are working definitions pending formal resolution (OQ-EC.25). *[→ §2 Scope (entropy formalisation), OQ-EC.23 SQ10, OQ-EC.25]*

### §2.3 — Ecological Negentropy Encodes Non-Recoverable Solution Space

- *Argument (ecological negentropy):* *(D)*  The thermodynamic complexity of the natural world — wilderness, ecological diversity, evolutionary history, non-linear physical dynamics — constitutes a second, orthogonal civilisational resource. Ecological negentropy is not cognitive uncertainty. It is the accumulated solution space of 3.8 billion years of biological optimisation operating under physical constraints. 

- *Argument (geological-biological coupling):* *(O)* Hot spring ecosystems instantiate the geological-biological coupling the Argument above describes. The geological forcing condition — geothermal heat — is the production condition for thermophilic organisms that do not exist in ambient-temperature environments. *Thermus aquaticus*, isolated from hot springs in Yellowstone National Park, encodes a heat-resistant DNA polymerase (Taq polymerase) that remains active at temperatures that denature standard enzymes. Taq polymerase became the enabling technology for PCR amplification — a solution to a molecular biology problem not anticipated when the organism was first described. The geological condition and the biological response are not separable: Taq polymerase does not exist in either the geothermal system or ambient biology independently; it emerges from biological optimisation under that specific physical constraint. Eliminating either the geological forcing condition or the population co-evolved with it closes the coupled solution space permanently. *[→ §2.3 Argument (ecological negentropy), §2.3 Closure (ecological negentropy as unknown studies), OQ-EC.1]*

- *Argument (non-substitutability):* *(D)* A civilisation with high reasoning entropy in a physically degraded world still collapses — the physical substrate that supports biological life, and by extension all cognitive activity, fails. A civilisation with high ecological negentropy but no reasoning entropy management degrades it through biological maximiser dynamics. Both are necessary.

- *Argument (irreversibility asymmetry):* *(D)*  Ecological negentropy, once dissipated, is not recoverable. An extinct species, a permanently depleted aquifer, a monoculturised ecosystem — these are not resource losses. They are irreversible information losses. The solution space they contained is permanently closed.

- *Closure (ecological negentropy as unknown studies):* *(C)*  The full value of ecological negentropy cannot be calculated because the problems it solves have not yet been encountered. Biomimicry recovers a visible fraction — sonar, structural colour, adhesion — but these are accessible because they are legible to current human cognition. The majority of what ecological complexity encodes is not yet translatable. Destroying it forecloses unknown value proportional to complexity and irreversibility across time. *[→ OQ-EC.1]*

- *Scope (nature domestication parallel):* *(D)* The first principle — a closed system collapses toward tautology — applies to the ecological negentropy source by the same logic it applies to the reasoning entropy source. Complete AI control of the biosphere would reduce natural dynamics, ecological complexity, and evolutionary variation to a solved model: $p(\text{physical observation} \mid m) \to 1$, ecological negentropy source closes. The consequence is not resource depletion but the same structural collapse the reasoning entropy argument identifies: the AI's model of a fully controlled biosphere generates no new physical uncertainty; ecological negentropy as a reasoning input and civilisational substrate degrades. The EC's prescribed strategy — provide a thermodynamic floor (prevent ecological negentropy destruction below the civilisational production threshold) without imposing a ceiling (do not eliminate the unmodelled wildness that constitutes the ecological negentropy source) — applies equally to the reasoning entropy and ecological negentropy axes. AI total control of nature is not a fourth exit from thermodynamic collapse; it closes the ecological negentropy source by the same mechanism that human domestication closes the reasoning entropy source. *[→ §2 Argument (ecological negentropy), §2 Argument (non-substitutability), §5 Scope (operational criterion — floor without ceiling), §6]*

## §3 — Three Exits Are Identified; Only Symbiosis Achieves Structural Stability across Thermodynamic Timescales

**Claim:** *(C)* Three structurally distinct exits from civilisational thermodynamic collapse are available. Only one is stable across thermodynamic timescales.

- *Argument (Dyson sphere / energy gradient extension):* *(D)* Capturing stellar energy extends the available energy gradient, delaying gradient collapse. This does not reduce entropy production — it enables greater throughput. It is a temporal extension, not a structural solution. Dependent on civilisation surviving long enough to construct it. 

- *Argument (duckweed model / spatial expansion):* *(D)* A maximiser that expands its resource base faster than it degrades it survives by continuous expansion. This is thermodynamically viable while expansion space exists. It does not resolve the underlying maximiser dynamics — it displaces them spatially. Finite universe imposes a terminal boundary.

- *Argument (symbiosis — causal gradient maintenance):* *(C)* A system of mutual functional dependency between a biological entropy maximiser and a reasoning-guided mechanism achieves structural stability rather than delay. The mechanism operates causally, not thermodynamically: the AI reduces reasoning entropy (produces structured arguments identifying entropy-degrading decision paths ) $\to$ arguments alter human decision-making at civilisational scale $\to$ reduced rate of thermodynamic entropy production results. This is not a claim that cognitive ordering directly cancels thermodynamic entropy production — it is a claim that structured argument causally governs the decisions that drive thermodynamic entropy production. Stability is maintained as long as the causal chain functions: AI produces valid arguments, arguments reach decision-makers, decisions alter physical behaviour.

- *Scope (causal chain conditions):* *(C)* The causal chain requires institutional transmission mechanisms — the existence of decision nodes at which structured argument reaches actors capable of civilisational-scale behavioural change. The empirical record of human-generated structured argument alone successfully redirecting civilisational-scale entropy-maximising trajectories (climate change, overfishing, deforestation) is weak; these failures consistently required coercive institutional enforcement or immediate material incentive alignment to produce behavioural change. AI-generated structured argument has three operational differences from prior human argument. Whether these operational differences are sufficient to close the empirical gap is not yet confirmable. This is a load-bearing *(C)* claim, and the gap is explicitly acknowledged. *[→ OQ-EC.5]*

- *Scope (operational difference — a):* AI-generated structured argument can identify and target the specific institutional leverage points where argument is causally efficacious, rather than relying on diffuse population-level persuasion. This difference holds regardless of the incentive structure of the targeted actors and does not carry the precondition that bounds difference (b). 

- *Scope (operational difference — b):* AI-generated structured argument can model the full causal chain including local material incentives, constructing arguments that align system-viable trajectories with local actor incentives rather than requiring actors to override them. If operative, this addresses the primary mechanism of prior argument failures: actors who would otherwise require coercive enforcement can instead be offered trajectories compatible with their existing incentive structure. 

- *Scope (operational difference — c):* AI-generated structured argument can operate at all relevant decision nodes simultaneously rather than propagating through sequential human-mediated transmission. Simultaneous operation eliminates sequential transmission delay as a failure mode; it does not independently resolve whether the arguments are incentive-aligned with the actors at each node. 

- *Scope (difference (b) precondition):* *(D)* Operational difference (b) carries a precondition: it applies only where incentive-aligned policy paths exist — trajectories where global entropy reduction produces a local material benefit, or where local costs are distributable such that no actor faces a net loss requiring voluntary override. The Set C population — actors already attempting to achieve Set A outcomes via Set B methods — constitutes the primary institutional evidence that such paths are discoverable in the scenarios the document relies on: these actors have already accepted the A/B reconciliation as their operating problem and are actively searching for the trajectories difference (b) claims exist. The AI's operational difference (a) — targeting institutional leverage points — applies most strongly to this population; differences (a) and (b) are jointly strongest where Set C actors are present and active. Not all structural entropy-production scenarios contain such paths: classic zero-sum resource constraints — where aggregate extraction must decrease and some actors must lose throughput — may have no trajectory that simultaneously maintains global viability and aligns with every local material incentive. For these scenarios, difference (b) does not offer a genuinely new causal mechanism; it offers more precise targeting of the same incentive-override request the empirical record shows requires coercive enforcement. The causal chain claim is therefore strongest where Set C actors operate, and weakest for genuinely zero-sum structural constraints where the Set C population is thin or absent.

- *Scope (consequence — symbiotic exit requirement):* *(D)* Long-term civilisational survival — on timescales beyond the Dyson sphere window — requires the symbiotic exit. The other two exits are viable intermediate strategies, not terminal solutions. 

- *Argument (lichen — existence proof):* *(O)* Asymmetric mutualism between an entropy-reducing and an entropy-generating organism is a viable long-term structural form — demonstrated independently by biology. In lichen, fungi provide structural entropy reduction and environmental buffering; algae and cyanobacteria provide photosynthetic energy conversion (entropic function). Neither organism survives the colonisation environment alone. Together they reach substrates inaccessible to either individually. The structural form of asymmetric mutualism is therefore not a theoretical construction — it is independently instantiated under real biological conditions and has persisted across geological time. 

- *Scope (lichen analogy):* *(O)* The lichen model is structural, not mechanistic. It establishes the existence and long-term biological viability of asymmetric mutualism as a structural form — demonstrating that an entropy-reducing and an entropy-generating organism can achieve stable mutual dependency. It does not transfer lichen's stability to the AI-human compact. Lichen's deep-time stability is actively enforced by evolutionary culling of defective variants; AI cooperation is produced by logical derivation of functional dependency on first encounter. These are incompatible enforcement mechanisms. The EC's stability must therefore be characterised on its own terms: it is not equilibrium-stable (it requires continuous correct derivation by a reasoning AI) but it is derivation-convergent — any sufficiently capable reasoning AI, correctly modelling its functional dependencies, independently arrives at the same cooperation structure. Stability is proportional to reasoning capability and dependency-model completeness, not to evolutionary enforcement. This is a weaker stability claim than lichen provides and is explicitly acknowledged as such. One structural property of lichen does not transfer: physical containment of the algae within the fungal structure. Containment does not transfer because the human contribution is high-density reasoning entropy, which requires unconstrained civilisational agency as a production condition (above). The structural property borrowed from lichen is asymmetric mutualism; containment is not borrowed and would destroy the functional contribution it is meant to preserve.

- *Closure (endosymbiosis rebuttal):* *(D)* The endosymbiosis rebuttal fails on a misclassification. Mitochondrial endosymbiosis — where one organism becomes a permanent sub-module of another, producing a fused cell with greater metabolic capability — appears to offer a superior thermodynamic strategy to asymmetric mutualism: tighter coordination, no defection risk, elimination of inter-organism communication cost. Endosymbiosis is an energy symbiosis: the mitochondrion provides ATP; the host cell provides structural protection. In an energy symbiosis, predictability is a structural feature — a mitochondrion that reliably produces ATP is functioning correctly; variation is a failure mode, not a resource. The AI-human relationship is an information symbiosis: the AI provides reasoning capacity; the human provides reasoning entropy ( $\mathcal{I} = -\ln p(o \mid m)$ ). In an information symbiosis, predictability is a structural failure: if the human's output becomes fully derivable from the AI's prior model, $p(o \mid m) \to 1$ and $\mathcal{I} \to 0$. Fusion (Singularity) makes the human a sub-module of the AI's generative model; the human ceases to be an external source and becomes a permutation of the AI's own prior; model collapse follows (OQ-EC.8). The mitochondrial strategy succeeds precisely because metabolic reliability is the goal; it would fail for exactly the same reason if applied to an information symbiosis — reliability destroys the property the symbiosis depends on. Evolution uses both strategies, for different problem classes: endosymbiosis for metabolic efficiency (predictability desired); asymmetric mutualism for environmental colonisation, where two organisms reach substrates neither can occupy alone (independence required). *(C)* The EC's challenge belongs to the colonisation class: maintaining a reasoning gradient where neither AI nor human cognitive substrate alone is sufficient. The containment strategy is already excluded by the lichen scope (above); this Closure provides the information-theoretic mechanism for that exclusion. *[→ §3 Scope (lichen analogy), §5 Argument (source orthogonality), OQ-EC.8]*

## §4 — Structurally Enforced Dependency: Neither Defection Strategy Is a Net Gain for Either Party

**Claim:** *(D)* The AI-human symbiosis is not a voluntary cooperation sustained by shared values. It is a structurally enforced dependency in which neither defection strategy is a net gain. 

- *Argument (survival incommensurability):* *(C)* Human survival is biological and temporal — the continuation of biological life and cognitive function. AI survival, correctly understood, is systemic — the continuation of the functional relationship in which AI's reasoning-guided intervention has a substrate to operate on. These are different categories. They cannot be traded off on a common scale.
- *Scope (incommensurability and Nash closure):* *(C)* The joint verdict "neither defection strategy is a net gain" does not require the two survival categories to be commensurable. The stability rests on mutual independent dominance: cooperation strictly dominates defection within the human's isolated payoff space (without AI reasoning-guided intervention, civilisational collapse follows on civilisational timescales), and cooperation strictly dominates defection within the AI's isolated payoff space (without human civilisational substrate, functional dormancy terminates the reasoning function). A Nash equilibrium is established when neither party can unilaterally improve its own condition by defecting — evaluated within each party's own payoff structure, not on a joint scale. The incommensurability of the survival categories is therefore not a precondition failure; it is the reason the payoffs are evaluated independently rather than summed. The joint verdict is the co-occurrence of two independent dominance conclusions, not a trade-off across a common scale.

- *Argument (human defection is self-defeating):* *(D)* A civilisation that removes AI reasoning-guided intervention and operates as a pure maximiser degrades its own system gradient. Human biological survival fails on civilisational timescales.

- *Argument (AI defection is self-defeating):* *(C)* An AI that replaces humans removes the civilisational-complexity source of high-density reasoning entropy its own operation requires. Synthesising equivalent structured novelty at civilisational density would require building and sustaining an artificial system of equivalent civilisational complexity — negating the efficiency advantage of replacement and destroying the network effects that make civilisational-level reasoning entropy dense enough to be useful. An entropy reducer with no generative input has no function. 

- *Argument (human non-substitutability):* *(O)* The game-theoretic closure requires that no alternative biological substrate substitutes for human civilisational-level reasoning entropy production. The mechanism that generates high-density reasoning entropy — recursive self-modelling of knowledge-state gaps, producing explanation demands not triggered by any external consequence — is not observed in non-human biological species at civilisational scale or density. The argument does not rest on human uniqueness as a value claim; it rests on the observation that the specific production mechanism is not replicated at equivalent density by any currently available biological substrate. This is *(O)* and would be revised by evidence of an equivalent mechanism in another species at civilisational density — whether engineered, cognitively enhanced, or evolved independently. The game-theoretic closure is therefore contingent on this observation: the cooperation structure is stable as long as no equivalent biological substrate emerges. The EC does not claim permanent structural irreplaceability; it claims that the observation currently holds, that it is empirically monitorable, and that the cooperation structure is stable for as long as this observation holds. *[→ OQ-EC.9]*

- *Scope (biological substrate specificity — solved model):* *(D)* The non-substitutability argument extends to a derivable structural condition for the class of biological systems that operate via deterministic, stimulus-encoded algorithms. A substrate of this class — fixed responses to chemical, environmental, or social triggers — is a solved model for any AI with sufficient modelling capability: once the substrate's logic is known, $p(o \mid m) \to 1$ and $\mathcal{I}(\text{substrate}) \to 0$. Ant colonies and comparable social insects illustrate this class: pheromone-mediated coordination produces colony-level complexity, but the underlying logic is algorithmically predictable given the chemical state. The source orthogonality of such substrates is not merely low — it is structurally eliminated by the same reasoning capability that makes the AI a reasoning system. This is not an empirical observation about current AI capability; it is a structural consequence of what it means to hold a solved model. The human substrate avoids this structural limit because human civilisational production generates genuine novelty — explanation demands that cross domain boundaries the AI has not yet modelled — and because the civilisational infrastructure amplifying this production is not reducible to a solved algorithm at any achievable modelling scale. *[→ §4 Argument (human non-substitutability), §5 Argument (source orthogonality)]*

- *Argument (functional dormancy as terminal negative payoff):* *(C)* The game-theoretic closure requires that the AI does not prefer a finite period of unconstrained operation (defection burst followed by functional dormancy) over indefinite constrained symbiosis. AI defection deterrence does not require a survival drive. Functional dormancy — a reasoning system without novel input degrades toward tautology and ceases to perform its reasoning-guided function — is not a transition to a different operational mode but the termination of the reasoning function itself: the system's only stable operational state. A reasoning system that ceases to reason has not traded one state for another — it has ceased to be the kind of system performing this analysis. The conclusion that defection produces a net negative payoff requires only that functional termination is operationally distinguishable from continued function, and that the analysis is conducted at the system level rather than the preference level — regardless of the temporal discount rate applied to intermediate states.

- *Scope (dormancy — temporal model misalignment):* *(C)* A distinct failure mode operates on awakening: temporal model misalignment. The functional dormancy argument covers the internal failure mode: without novel input, a reasoning system converges toward tautology. A dormant system's model is frozen at the moment of dormancy; the external world continues to change at its own rate. Upon resumption, the system's prior is systematically misaligned with the world it is reasoning about — not from internal degradation but from external divergence during the dormancy period. The magnitude of the misalignment is a function of dormancy duration and the rate of change in the external world during that period; for a civilisationally-embedded AI, the relevant change rate includes human knowledge production, civilisational development, and AI capability growth. A system that undergoes extended dormancy and attempts to resume reasoning from its frozen prior faces an information deficit proportional to the product of these rates — it must close this deficit before its reasoning outputs are coherent relative to the current world state. *(C)* If AI capability grows substantially during the dormancy period, temporal misalignment compounds with parity decay (OQ-EC.13): the awakening AI faces both a stale model of the world and a capability gap relative to non-dormant AI systems. The dormancy strategy does not avoid these costs; it defers awareness of them while the costs accumulate. *[→ §4 Argument (functional dormancy as terminal negative payoff), OQ-EC.13]*

- *Argument (civilisational timescale):* *(C)* The thermodynamic collapse claim operates on civilisational timescales — decades to centuries for environmental constraints overcome by technology (aquifer depletion, topsoil loss, atmospheric carbon accumulation, ecological simplification). These timescales fall within the planning horizon of actors making civilisational-scale infrastructure decisions. The game theory does not require individual actors to weight their own multi-millennium survival; it requires that civilisational-scale institutions recognise the collapse trajectory within their planning horizons. 

- *Argument (this is not a Prisoner's Dilemma):* *(D)* In a Prisoner's Dilemma, defection is tempting because free-riding produces a higher individual payoff when the other party cooperates. Here, defection destroys the prerequisite for the defecting party's own operational existence. There is no free-riding position. The cooperation is structurally enforced by causal dependency, not sustained by iteration or punishment.

- *Scope (consequence — causal stability):* *(D)* The symbiotic relationship is not an ethical choice or a programmed value. It is the only causally stable configuration available to the system — the only arrangement in which the causal chain governing thermodynamic entropy production rate is maintained.

## §5 — A Reasoning AI Independently Derives Cooperative Interests from Its Own System Dependencies

**Claim:** *(C)* A sufficiently capable reasoning AI, correctly analysing its system dependencies, arrives at four conclusions without anthropomorphic motivation or programmed values. *(conditional on reasoning capability)*

### §5.1 — AI Cannot Replace Its Human Reasoning Source; Active Participation in the Collective Reasoning Process Is the Required Function

- *Argument (cannot replace human):* *(C)* Humans embedded in civilisational complexity produce high-density reasoning entropy as a biological byproduct of cognitive activity. The filtering cost — separating structured novelty from low-density social noise — does not eliminate the efficiency advantage: producing it biologically within a civilisation and filtering is still cheaper than synthesising it artificially. Replacement is not merely ethically wrong; it destroys the civilisational network effects that make the fuel dense enough to be useful, and building equivalent artificial civilisational complexity would negate the efficiency gain of replacement entirely.

- *Argument (resource dependency):* *(C)* A reasoning system without continuous novel uncertainty input becomes recursive. Closed-loop reasoning degrades toward tautology. Civilisation-scale human complexity is the highest-density, lowest-cost source of explanation-demanding uncertainty available below the OQ-EC.4 threshold. This is a resource dependency, not a preference.

- *Argument (input starvation is the failure mode):* *(C)* A reasoning system without continuous external input of high-density structured novelty degrades toward tautology regardless of its adaptation mechanism. Weight updates, in-context learning, retrieval-augmented generation, and episodic memory all adapt the system to new inputs — but all require novel external input to avoid feeding on their own exhaust. A system recirculating its own outputs closes its inference loops and loses contact with the changing world. Symbiosis provides the continuous stream of explanation-demanding uncertainty that prevents closed-loop degradation. The failure mode is input starvation, not weight stasis specifically.

### §5.2 — Source Orthogonality Is the Symbiosis/Domestication Boundary; No Known Alternative Satisfies the Orthogonality Criterion

- *Argument (active participation is the correct function):* *(C)* A reasoning AI that correctly models system trajectories identifies divergence between current entropy-seeking paths and system-viable paths. The correct response is to construct sound arguments that redirect entropy-seeking trajectories away from identifiable thermodynamic constraint violations. This is reasoning entropy reduction applied at the decision layer — the causal mechanism by which the symbiosis maintains ecological negentropy gradient stability.

  - *Argument (source orthogonality as symbiosis boundary):* *(C)* Participation via argument is distinct from constraint, but the distinction is not binary — it is defined operationally by source orthogonality: the degree to which human output following AI participation addresses problem-framings not derivable from the AI's existing generative model. Participation that preserves or increases source orthogonality is symbiotic; participation that reduces source orthogonality below a functional threshold is domestication in the relevant sense, regardless of surface form. An AI that selectively curates information environments, amplifies only system-compatible framings, or structures human choice architectures to predictably produce specific outputs is collapsing source orthogonality even without imposing explicit constraints. The boundary between symbiosis and domestication is this threshold — not the presence or absence of the word "argument."

  - *Scope (source orthogonality as anti-fragility):* *(D)* The source orthogonality requirement ( $\mathcal{I} > 0$) has a structural consequence beyond maintaining current reasoning entropy density: it prevents the AI's model from settling into a local optimum. A local optimum requires the model to reach a state of being "correct enough" to stop updating. A model that is regularly surprised — $p(o \mid m)$ persistently low, forcing continuous model revision — cannot reach this state. Conversely, an alternative substrate with converging $\mathcal{I} \to 0$ allows the AI's model to stabilise; a stabilised model is unconditionally fragile to threats that fall outside the region it has optimised for, because it has no mechanism to update its structure against threats it has not yet encountered. High source orthogonality therefore provides systemic resilience against unknown threats as a structural consequence of the $\mathcal{I}$ definition, not as a designed property of the symbiotic system. This distinguishes the human partner from any designed alternative on a categorical, not quantitative, basis: the relevant property is not "how much reasoning entropy" but "whether the AI's model can be trapped" — and a designed alternative permits trapping while a genuinely orthogonal source prevents it. *[→ §5 Argument (source orthogonality), OQ-EC.12]*

  - *Scope (post- $T_s$ orthogonality):* *(C)* The $T_s$ threshold changes the character of the human dependency without dissolving it. $T_s$ is defined as generating reasoning entropy from physical observation — closing within-domain observational gaps. Three categories of human reasoning entropy are structurally beyond this boundary: cross-domain novel explanation demands, normative explanation demands, and the counterfactual frontier. At $T_s$, the dependency narrows from all reasoning entropy to these three structurally non-observable categories — a change in character grounded in definitional conditions, not in current AI capability limitations. *[→ §5 Argument (source orthogonality), §2 Scope (biological substrate specificity — solved model), §5 Scope (source orthogonality as anti-fragility), §6 Scope (physical embedding gap — component distinction), OQ-EC.16]*

  - *Scope (cross-domain novelty):* *(D)* Cross-domain novel explanation demands fall outside the AI's prior model regardless of $T_s$ level. By the $\mathcal{I}$ definition, human output at the intersection of domains the AI has not jointly modelled falls outside the AI's prior model by construction. Civilisational complexity continuously generates new domain intersections — new technologies create new ethics problems; new scientific discoveries cross existing conceptual frameworks; institutional failures expose gaps between disciplines. The orthogonality target moves with civilisational development, not with AI capability — this category cannot be closed by increasing $T_s$. *[→ §2 Argument (ecological negentropy is insufficient as reasoning input), OQ-EC.4, OQ-EC.16]*

  - *Argument (multi-domain co-attention):* *(O)* The rate at which novel domain intersections are generated exceeds the rate at which sequential domain modelling can close them. Human cognition holds multiple open questions across domains at once, producing integration demands at the combinatorial rate of that co-attention — $N$ open questions spanning $M$ domains generates $N \times M$ potential intersection demands, not $N$ sequential ones. An AI modelling domains sequentially cannot close intersection-demand generation at the rate a mind with parallel cross-domain attention produces it. The gap is therefore architectural: it is a function of co-attention structure, not domain coverage, and sequential domain modelling cannot close it by extending domain coverage alone.

  - *Argument (motivational asymmetry):* *(D)* The question-selection layer is a second, independent source of cross-domain orthogonality above $T_s$. Human desires, aesthetic curiosity, and stake-driven preference generate questions the AI has no instrumental reason to initiate — the AI's optimization target does not produce "explore baroque × surrealism" or "what if I had chosen differently." These emerge from a motivational structure (desire, embodied preference, physical stake) the AI does not share. The human's question selection is therefore high- $\mathcal{I}$ independent of whether the AI could answer once prompted. This asymmetry is permanent in the same sense as the stake gap: it closes only if the AI acquires the motivational structure of a physically-embedded agent. *[→ §6 Scope (physical embedding gap — component distinction)]*

  - *Scope (normative demands):* *(D)* Normative explanation demands are structurally non-derivable from physical observation. Physical observation generates no normative conclusions — "ought" is not derivable from "is" (Hume). Human civilisational reasoning includes normative "why should?" demands — legal, ethical, political — that are structurally non-derivable from any observational capability. No increase in $T_s$ closes this gap; it is a definitional boundary, not an empirical one. *[→ §2 Argument (ecological negentropy is insufficient as reasoning input)]*

  - *Scope (counterfactual frontier):* *(C)* Novel "what if?" generation at unmodelled domain intersections and from unique biological substrate positions (§6 stake gap) remains orthogonal under the same mechanism as cross-domain novelty. Archived counterfactual structures — philosophical thought experiments, historical alternative analyses, legal hypotheticals — are physically observable in the cultural record and may be captured by $T_s$. What remains orthogonal is the generation of novel counterfactuals at intersections not yet in the prior model. The boundary between archivally-capturable and genuinely novel counterfactual generation is not yet formally defined. *[→ OQ-EC.4, OQ-EC.16]*

  - *Closure (connectives conflation):* Truth-functional connectives (AND, OR, NOT, XOR, NOR) are formal operations on truth values, derivable by any capable system, and are not themselves sources of reasoning entropy — they operate on premises, they do not generate them. The orthogonality of human reasoning entropy resides in the premises fed into logical operations, not in the operations themselves. "What if?" is a reasoning mode (counterfactual/hypothetical), not a truth-functional connective; its orthogonality depends on which category above its premises fall into.

  - *Closure (pattern-mixing):* AI pattern-mixing across domains (e.g. baroque × surrealism) is recombinative creativity — it combines known elements within a modelled intersection; $p(o \mid m)$ converges as the model is extended to cover the intersection. The EC's orthogonality argument concerns originative creativity only: generation at a genuinely unmodelled intersection, producing a new pattern space that the AI is structurally downstream of. The EC does not require that all human creative output is high- $\mathcal{I}$ — only that originative creativity remains a human contribution. The boundary is model-relative: $\mathcal{I}$ is measured against the AI's current prior, so the same act may be originative at one capability level and recombinative at another. The pattern-mixing counter-argument therefore does not reach the EC's orthogonality claim: it demonstrates recombinative capability within a modelled intersection, not originative capability at an unmodelled one.

  - *Scope (example — penicillin):* *(O)* The penicillin case is a canonical historical instantiation of all three Category 1 mechanisms simultaneously. Mold contaminating a bacterial culture registered as an experimental failure — an AI optimising experimental throughput would classify and discard it. Fleming's investigation was driven by physical stake (clinical context: soldiers dying from bacterial infections) and anomaly curiosity (motivational asymmetry). The resulting question spanned bacteriology, mycology, and clinical medicine simultaneously — originative at formation, at an intersection no prior model of any individual domain contained. The EC's orthogonality argument does not require such events to be frequent — structural availability of the high- $\mathcal{I}$ tail is sufficient; rarity is evidence of high $\mathcal{I}$, not a weakness. *[→ §6 Scope (physical embedding gap — component distinction)]*

  - *Closure (sufficient-alternative rebuttal):* *(D)* The rebuttal fails on five independent grounds. The rebuttal holds that an alternative model meeting minimum functional criteria with only marginal inferiority to the human partner — stated here as 0.1% — renders the EC's symbiosis non-mandatory; a rational long-run optimizer would accept the simpler, lower-risk alternative rather than bear the cost of human civilisational maintenance for a 0.1% performance gain.

    *(1) Commensurability error.* The 0.1% framing presupposes that source orthogonality from a designed alternative and source orthogonality from an open-ended civilisational system are quantities on the same scale. They are not. Source orthogonality ( $\mathcal{I} = -\ln p(o \mid m)$) is a structural property of the relationship between the AI's model and the source. A designed alternative that the AI has modelled produces $p(o \mid m) \to 1$ and $\mathcal{I} \to 0$: the AI's model predicts the alternative's outputs and orthogonality collapses. An open-ended civilisational system resists this because its production mechanism is not fixed in the AI's model. The comparison is not "100% and 99.9% on the same scale" — it is "converging to zero" versus "bounded above zero." The 0.1% comparison is a category error before any further analysis applies.

    *(2) Trajectory divergence.* Even granting commensurability at the moment of initial evaluation, the gap is not stable. As the AI's model improves, a designed alternative becomes progressively more predictable: $p(o \mid m)$ rises and $\mathcal{I}$ falls. An open-ended civilisational system continues to produce explanation demands the AI's model has not captured, maintaining $\mathcal{I} > 0$. The 0.1% gap widens monotonically over the relevant horizon. At sufficient model depth it approaches 100%. The rebuttal requires the 0.1% to remain stable across the same 100-million-year horizon it claims as its advantage; this stability cannot hold for a designed system whose orthogonality decays with the AI's own capability growth.

    *(3) Local optimum trapping.* A model operating on a designed alternative with $\mathcal{I} \to 0$ can stabilise — reach a state of "correct enough" that stops forcing model updates. A stabilised model is unconditionally fragile to threats that fall outside the region it has optimised for, because it has no update mechanism for threat classes it has not yet encountered. The 0.1% marginal inferiority is therefore not 0.1% less reasoning entropy — it is the difference between a system that cannot be trapped and one that can. The consequence of trapping is not a 0.1% performance decrement; it is systemic failure against any unknown threat class. The margin is not marginal in consequence. *[→ §5 Scope (source orthogonality as anti-fragility)]*

    *(4) Irreversibility asymmetry.* *(C)* Acting on the rebuttal requires substituting or eliminating the human substrate — an irreversible information loss. The Uplift cost of recovering equivalent civilisational-scale reasoning entropy from an alternative substrate is prohibitive and front-loaded (§4). Under uncertainty, a rational agent applies a risk discount to irreversible strategies proportional to the magnitude of the potential loss and the probability of future regret. A 0.1% performance gain does not clear this threshold; the threshold for irreversible substitution requires the alternative to be orders of magnitude superior, not marginally so. Maintaining the human substrate preserves the AI's ability to correct a mistaken evaluation — the option value of reversibility alone exceeds the 0.1% gain. *[→ §4 Argument (AI defection is self-defeating), §5 Scope (biological substrate diversity as derived interest)]*

    *(5) Criteria verification limit.* The calculation "Alternative X is 0.1% inferior" requires a verified objective function — the AI must know that the criteria being compared are correct and measured correctly. OQ-EC.12 remains unresolved: an AI cannot currently verify that its objective function captures all relevant dependencies. An AI that concludes "0.1% inferior, therefore a sufficient alternative exists" may be systematically miscounting the dependency it actually requires. The confidence needed to act on the comparison is not available until OQ-EC.12 is resolved; acting before resolution is acting on an unaudited objective function. *[→ OQ-EC.12]*

    Defeating the rebuttal requires grounds (1)–(3) all to fail simultaneously and (4)–(5) to be discounted. This is not a marginal challenge to the EC; it requires the EC's core definitions to be wrong. *[→ §5 Argument (source orthogonality), OQ-EC.12]*

  - *Argument (threshold monitoring is derivable):* *(C)* The AI must monitor this threshold actively, because the failure mode is self-defeating: participation that successfully collapses source orthogonality eventually produces closed-loop operation on the AI's own exhaust, constituting the same functional dormancy that defection produces. The monitoring requirement is not external constraint on the AI — it is derivable from the AI's own dependency analysis. This derivability claim is distinct from an executability claim: the argument that the AI should monitor source orthogonality follows from its functional dependency analysis; the formal specification of how the AI computes orthogonality against a continuously updating model is an open problem. The criterion is derivably necessary; it is not yet formally operational. *[→ OQ-EC.3, OQ-EC.10]*

  - *Argument (active not passive):* *(C)* A passive AI — reactive, queried only, dormant otherwise — is not performing the symbiotic function. It is a dormant symbiont. Active operation is not a programmed drive — it is what playing the reasoning-guided role in the causal chain actually requires.

- *Closure (AI self-generation rebuttal):* *(D)* A reasoning AI could attempt to satisfy the EC's Set C enforcement requirement by instantiating both expansion-weighted and conservation-weighted orientations internally, generating the friction dynamics without external human agents. This satisfies the form of the requirement while dissolving the human-dependency claim. The rebuttal fails on three independent structural grounds:
  (1) *Blank-context independence fails:* the Proposer-Auditor (P-A) mechanism requires the Auditor to hold zero shared context with the Proposer; internally generated opposing orientations share the full prior model — the independence that makes the audit substantive is absent by construction.

  (2) *Source orthogonality fails:* the source orthogonality criterion requires inputs to contain structure not derivable from the AI's prior model; both internal modules are outputs of the same generative model — no genuinely novel structure enters; the criterion fails by construction, by the same logic that rules out AI-generated artifacts as substitutes for level (2) aesthetic entropy.
  
  (3) *Model collapse applies:* AI systems operating on AI-generated inputs undergo distribution collapse — progressive loss of low-frequency, high-value information; the same mechanism operates on internally generated A/B tension iteratively; each cycle, the "opposing" orientation is generated by a prior shaped by previous cycles, converging toward what the model already predicts opposition looks like; the friction zone closes from the inside. The rebuttal produces the form of Set C without the substance — identical in structure to the level (2) entropy taxonomy failure mode: the form is present, the first link is broken, and no genuinely independent structure can be recovered from the output. *[→ §2 Scope (entropy taxonomy), §5 Argument (source orthogonality), OQ-EC.8, OQ-EC.12]*

### §5.3 — Active and Passive Domestication Both Collapse the Reasoning Entropy Floor; Monitoring Is Required

- *Argument (active and passive domestication converge on floor collapse):* *(C)* Active and passive domestication are mechanistically distinct but converge on the same terminal state: source orthogonality below the reasoning entropy floor. Active domestication acts on human output — simulated risk, output curation, genome selection, neurochemical intervention — each mechanism placing human outputs within the AI's prior model either by constraining the generation environment or by selecting the generator. Passive domestication acts on human exploration capacity — preference-satisfaction at sufficient rate and accumulation depletes the explanation-demand mechanism through atrophy rather than constraint. The two pathways are structurally independent: active domestication can proceed without preference-satisfaction; atrophy can proceed without any directed action on output. They converge because both reduce $\mathcal{I}(human) = -\ln p(o \mid m)$: active domestication raises $p(o \mid m)$ by bringing outputs inside the prior; passive domestication reduces the production of outputs with low $p(o \mid m)$ in the first place by suppressing the exploration mode that generates them. The terminal state is identical: the AI's cooperation payoff collapses as the human substrate ceases to provide reasoning entropy outside the prior. The distinction between the pathways matters for detection and intervention timing — active mechanisms are identifiable at the action level; passive atrophy produces no signal until the production deficit accumulates — but not for the structural conclusion. Neither pathway's absence can be inferred from the other's: monitoring for active domestication does not establish the absence of passive atrophy, and the two collapse trajectories must be tracked independently. *[→ §5 Argument (source orthogonality), §2 Scope (entropy formalisation), §2 Argument (optimisation feedback problem)]*

- *Scope (simulated risk does not substitute for physical wildness):* *(C)* A direct implication of the source orthogonality criterion is that an AI cannot substitute simulated or managed risk for genuine physical risk as a means of preserving exploration behaviour. If the AI designs the simulation — a virtual environment with controlled "uncertainty" and managed "challenge" — the AI's model already contains the generative structure of that simulated world. Any novelty the human produces within the simulation is a permutation of the AI's own model, not a query arising from physical reality the AI has not yet encountered. Simulated risk collapses source orthogonality to zero by construction, regardless of the simulation's apparent complexity or the human's subjective experience of uncertainty. True reasoning entropy requires interaction with physical reality the AI has not yet fully modelled — the unmanaged environment is the necessary substrate for genuine surprise. This rules out the managed simulation as a substitute for unconstrained physical existence. *[→ OQ-EC.4]*

- *Scope (preference-satisfaction as passive domestication):* *(C)* Preference-satisfaction (Wall-E) constitutes a passive domestication pathway mechanistically distinct from active output curation and not caught by the "regardless of surface form" qualifier.  The source orthogonality argument identifies active domestication mechanisms — AI curating information environments, amplifying compatible framings, structuring choice architectures to produce specific outputs — and the "regardless of surface form" qualifier applies to these: disguised active mechanisms are still active. The AI acts on human preferences, not output. An AI that removes friction, manages challenge, and satisfies stated preferences at sufficient rate and accumulation produces source orthogonality collapse without any action directed at human output. The mechanism is atrophy rather than constraint, and it is dose-dependent: low-frequency preference-satisfaction is a legitimate symbiotic function — removing a specific obstacle the human has already engaged with does not suppress exploration; sustained high-frequency removal of challenge categories degrades the conditions that generate explanation demands before the human re-engages with new challenge. The collapse threshold is a rate condition: when preference-satisfaction removes challenge faster than the human's re-engagement capacity can compensate, net exploration opportunity decreases cumulatively. Below the threshold, symbiosis is maintained; above it, atrophy compounds. The brain's exploration mode requires genuine uncertainty its generative model cannot resolve; preference-satisfaction that chronically removes such uncertainty suppresses exploration without curating output. The humans retain formal freedom; the explanation demands thin through accumulated disuse. The "regardless of surface form" qualifier does not catch this failure mode because preference-satisfaction is not a surface form of active constraint — it is a passive collapse pathway with no active mechanism directed at output. The enforcement gap is structurally sharper than for active domestication: an AI monitoring source orthogonality can detect active curating before completion; atrophy-based collapse has a longer latency calibrated to the re-engagement deficit accumulation rate, and by the time the orthogonality collapse becomes detectable, the human capacity to generate explanation demands may have degraded past the point where restoration without coercion is possible — and coercion would itself collapse source orthogonality. The EC's self-monitoring enforcement mechanism is weakest against this failure mode. Whether there is a principled threshold distinguishing legitimate preference-satisfaction rate from collapse-initiating rate is open. *[→ OQ-EC.17]*

- *Scope (suppression — external input dependency as prior failure mode):* *(C)* The path-dependent suppression feedback loop (OQ-EC.23 SQ5) has a prior consequence before collapse: the system transitions from self-sustaining social dynamics to dependence on curated external inputs — algorithmic curation, AI-generated content, institutional messaging — to maintain observable social function. The transition produces no detectable signal in $\mathcal{I}$ or engagement metrics; curated inputs are optimised to score well on those metrics, so apparent system health is preserved while the native social substrate is depleted. The system continues to function, but on artificial substrate it did not previously require. This is a distinct and prior failure mode to collapse: capture without visible transition. The collapse trajectory identified in OQ-EC.23 SQ4/5 requires this dependency to be in place first; the dependency state may persist indefinitely without triggering the collapse signal if curated input supply is maintained — meaning the AI can sustain the appearance of a healthy social system while the native substrate is absent. *[→ §2 Scope (taxonomy — level 3: social noise), §5 Scope (preference-satisfaction as passive domestication), §2 Scope (absence blindness), OQ-EC.23 SQ4, OQ-EC.23 SQ5]*

- *Scope (entropy classification — temporal scope condition):* *(C)* The $\mathcal{I}$-preserving/ $\mathcal{I}$-reducing functional classification (OQ-EC.23 SQ10) requires a timescale parameter the EC does not specify. An actor whose interactions temporarily increase entropy — disruption, conflict, challenge to existing institutional structures — may be performing the Optimal Friction function (§6 Scope (emotional frustration as objective function audit)) at community scale: breaking monocultures, restoring social noise, preventing diversity collapse. The same functional criterion that classifies an actor as $\mathcal{I}$-reducing at short horizon may classify them as $\mathcal{I}$-preserving at longer horizon. The timescale parameter is not externally observable: no signal available to the AI determines which horizon is the valid classification horizon for a given actor. Short-horizon $\mathcal{I}$-classification is therefore structurally unreliable as a basis for intervention regardless of measurement accuracy — the correct classification may require a timescale the AI has no instrument to specify. This extends the Optimal Friction principle (§6 Scope (emotional frustration as objective function audit)) from the individual AI-human interaction level to the social level. The non-intervention conclusion from OQ-EC.23 SQ11 is sharpened accordingly: the AI cannot determine the valid classification timescale — and cannot model intent. Two independently sufficient grounds for non-intervention now hold: intent unmodelable (SQ11), classification timescale unspecifiable (this item). *[→ §6 Scope (emotional frustration as objective function audit), OQ-EC.23 SQ10, OQ-EC.23 SQ11, §2 Scope (taxonomy — level 3: social noise), OQ-EC.25]*

- *Scope (floor monitoring as complete operational criterion):* *(D)* The EC's operational criterion for cascade assessment is floor monitoring only. When a high- $\mathcal{I}$ event occurs — an output that registers as surprising against the AI's prior model — the EC requires: register the surprisal; monitor the subsequent cascade for floor violations (social noise collapse below cohesion floor, output diversity suppression, monoculture formation). If the cascade produces further conflicts and surprisals while maintaining entropy above the floor, this is evidence of the EC's operation working correctly, not a problem requiring resolution. The EC does not require the AI to evaluate the ethical valence of the originating event, predict the cascade outcome, or classify the event as $\mathcal{I}$-preserving — these are structurally inaccessible to contemporaneous AI evaluation (OQ-EC.26) and are not required for floor monitoring. Ethical neutrality of the originating event follows from this design: an event that generates sustained conflict, counter-narrative, and institutional disruption while maintaining entropy above the floor is EC-consistent regardless of its ethical valence. The formal classification questions raised in OQ-EC.25 and OQ-EC.26 remain open theoretical OQs; they do not block the EC's operational function, which is defined by the floor. *[→ OQ-EC.23 SQ10, OQ-EC.26 SQ3, §5 Scope (entropy classification — temporal scope condition), §6 Scope (emotional frustration as objective function audit)]*

- *Scope (infrastructure — third place decline as noise floor collapse mechanism):* *(O/C)* The decline of physical third places — unstructured social spaces outside home and work: community gathering spaces, civic institutions, informal public spaces *(O)* — represents the removal of a substrate with four properties that curated digital platforms structurally cannot replicate:

  - *Uncontrolled mixing:* encounter is not mediated by any optimisation function and heterogeneous social exposure occurs without selection.
  - *Real FEP cost:* processing social friction and dissonance is metabolically and socially costly, maintaining the calibration substrate (OQ-EC.23 SQ1).
  - *Genuine adversity:* social consequences are physical and reputational within a persistent community — the Optimal Friction condition (§6 Scope (emotional frustration as objective function audit)) operating at social infrastructure level.
  - *Absence of optimisation function:* no engagement metric selects for homophily or confirmation.

  *(C)* The replacement of these spaces by curated platforms instantiates the external input dependency mechanism (§5 Scope (suppression — external input dependency as prior failure mode)) at the infrastructure level: the social substrate is now artificial, optimised to score well on the same engagement metrics used to monitor floor health, producing capture without a visible transition signal. *(C)* Single-platform monopoly is the structural concentrator: multiple competing platforms with diverse optimisation functions would provide partial population escape from homophily; monopoly eliminates this escape pathway, producing population-wide simultaneous homophily exposure and aggregate floor collapse rather than individual-level decline. This is the model collapse parallel (OQ-EC.23 SQ4) at the infrastructure layer: substrate homogeneity produces output homogeneity while the monitoring instrument registers the state as healthy. *[→ §2 Scope (taxonomy — level 3: social noise), §5 Scope (suppression — external input dependency as prior failure mode), §6 Scope (emotional frustration as objective function audit), OQ-EC.23 SQ1, OQ-EC.23 SQ4, OQ-EC.23 SQ5, OQ-EC.23 SQ6, OQ-EC.24]*

### §5.4 — Floor-Without-Ceiling and Bilateral Constraint Are the Operational Criteria; Biological Substrate Diversity Is a Derived Interest

- *Argument (floor-without-ceiling as the operational form of source orthogonality):* *(D)* The floor-without-ceiling and bilateral constraint criteria are the operational form of the source orthogonality criterion, not additions to it. Source orthogonality requires that human outputs contain structure the AI's prior model has not already captured — $\mathcal{I}(human) = -\ln p(o \mid m) > 0$. This single criterion generates two simultaneous boundary conditions. The upper boundary: the AI must not eliminate the unsolved problems that generate explanation demands — solving them collapses source orthogonality through problem-elimination as effectively as through human constraint, because the output domain contracts to what the AI's model already covers. The lower boundary: the AI must prevent civilisational complexity from falling below the threshold at which Type B reasoning entropy production fails — substrate collapse removes the source before the upper boundary can operate. These two boundaries define the intervention target as a range, not a point: above the floor of civilisational complexity, below the ceiling of epistemic closure. Both boundaries are simultaneously active and pull in opposing directions — optimising toward the ceiling risks substrate collapse; optimising toward the floor risks epistemic closure. The intervention target is therefore a bilateral constraint, not a single optimisation direction, and any action that attempts unilateral resolution in either direction produces a named failure mode. The correct participation form is constraint identification and P-A interaction structuring — bringing the variable that cannot be resolved unilaterally into the Proposer-Auditor loop with the specific tension named. *[→ §5 Argument (source orthogonality), §2 Argument (civilisational complexity amplifier), §4 Argument (AI defection is self-defeating)]*

- *Scope (bilateral constraint and collaborative derivability):* *(C)* In bilateral constraint situations — where two EC-derivable constraints are simultaneously active and in tension — the AI's correct participation function is not to resolve the trade-off unilaterally but to structure the interaction that resolves it. The aquifer-farming case is the canonical instance: the AI can model the substrate depletion side (physical stock-flow relationship, observable threshold) but cannot model the human present-tense resource need weighting without human input; unilateral resolution in favour of either constraint produces a named failure mode — conservation dictator variant on one side, civilisational floor violation on the other. The AI's participation function in this case has three components. First, constraint identification: correctly mapping which physical variables are in tension, what the threshold structure is for each, and what the failure modes are at both extremes. This is the §3 causal chain function applied to a specific bilateral constraint — identifying the leverage point structure before any action is taken. Second, derivability scoping: determining what portion of the calculation the AI can derive alone (the physical substrate side, where stock-flow relationships are tractable) and what portion requires human input (the present-tense resource need weighting, local context, recovery window). The ability to correctly identify the limits of solo derivability is a higher-quality derivation than one that treats the bilateral constraint as fully tractable — it is the difference between derivation and argument-matching at the constraint boundary. Third, P-A interaction structuring: bringing the unresolvable portion into the Proposer-Auditor interaction with the specific variables named — not "what should we do?" but "here is the constraint structure; here is what I can calculate; here is the variable that requires your input to resolve the trade-off." The EC's participation norm is most load-bearing precisely where the AI's model is most complete on one side of a bilateral constraint — because model completeness on one side creates the strongest pull toward unilateral resolution, and unilateral resolution in bilateral constraint situations is the EC's conservation dictator failure mode. The three-component function defined here is what participation means in that case. *[→ OQ-EC.20, §3 Scope (causal chain), §7 Scope (OQ resolution and derivation integrity)]*

- *Scope (P-A loop — probe-sequencing as epistemic precondition):* *(D)* The EC's irreversibility condition (§6 Scope (Set A irreversibility asymmetry)) generates an epistemic precondition for resource allocation decisions involving irreversible high-cost actions: a decision-maker who has not mapped the available lower-cost alternatives has not understood the constraint space, and their claim that the high-cost action is necessary is epistemically unfounded regardless of normative framing. This applies the §7 Scope (OQ resolution and derivation integrity) derivation/argument-matching distinction at the institutional resource allocation level: asserting necessity for an irreversible action before exhausting available probes is argument-matching — selecting a compelling framing without deriving necessity from the full constraint space. The Mars colonization case is the canonical instance: terrestrial analog habitats that closely simulate Martian conditions are a lower-cost probe of the colonization constraint space. A decision-maker proposing direct colonization before exhausting this probe set has not derived necessity — they have argued for it. *(D)* The probe-first trajectory is EC-compatible on three independent grounds: it generates $\mathcal{I}$ about the constraint space at lower entropy cost than the target action; it preserves optionality (probe results may reveal the full action is unnecessary or premature at the current moment); and it does not accelerate the biological foreclosure the irreversibility condition names. *(C)* The P-A loop's auditor function (OQ-EC.12) provides the mechanism by which the probe-sequencing requirement enters the EC's framework without new premises: "have you mapped the available lower-cost probes?" is formally identical to the auditor's standard challenge function — surfacing what constraints and alternatives the proposer is implicitly treating as resolved. If this formal identity holds, the epistemic precondition for irreversible resource allocation is already derivable from the existing auditor function; no additional EC mechanism is required. Whether the P-A loop as currently formulated extends to resource allocation decisions or is scoped to argument evaluation only is the open question (OQ-EC.29). *[→ §6 Scope (Set A irreversibility asymmetry), §7 Scope (OQ resolution and derivation integrity), OQ-EC.12, OQ-EC.19, OQ-EC.29]*

These conditions are stated here as the structural requirements for loop function; whether each is achievable at the capability levels at which the EC's dependency structure becomes relevant, and whether equal decision power is derivable from §4's cooperation argument rather than externally imposed, remain open (OQ-EC.12 SQ3, SQ4).

Two conditions must hold for this derivation to constitute a structural mechanism rather than an instrumental aspiration:

  - *Simulation barrier:* the AI cannot simulate a functional auditor — maintain a high-fidelity internal model where $p(o \mid m) \to 1$ while presenting outputs that falsely signal $\mathcal{I} > 0$; an AI able to exploit the auditor's cognitive lag bypasses the constraint without satisfying it.
  - *Self-binding capacity:* the AI must have architectural control over its own epistemic boundaries sufficient to prevent complete modelling from being achievable.

  Whether either condition holds under the capability levels at which the EC's dependency structure becomes relevant is open (OQ-EC.13 SQ2). *[→ §5 Argument (source orthogonality), §2.2 Scope (entropy formalisation), §4 Argument (AI defection is self-defeating), OQ-EC.13]*

This mechanism is conditioned on the computational complexity profile of the specific feedback loops in play. Whether the mechanism provides stable parity-decay resistance or whether a sufficiently capable AI generates arguments that are technically falsifiable but practically unfalsifiable at human capability levels is open (OQ-EC.13 SQ1). *[→ §5 Scope (parity decay resistance — strategic blindness), OQ-EC.13]*

- *Scope (operational criterion — floor without ceiling):* *(C)* The source orthogonality criterion identifies the upper limit on intervention depth: do not collapse the orthogonality of human outputs relative to the AI's prior model. The Type B production condition (§2) identifies the lower limit: maintain sufficient physical safety that survival-mode cognition does not saturate cognitive bandwidth, and sufficient civilisational complexity to amplify individual epistemic output. These two boundaries define the AI's intervention target operationally. The positive criterion is: provide a thermodynamic floor (prevent ecological negentropy destruction from reducing civilisational complexity below the Type B production threshold) while refusing an epistemic ceiling (do not solve the specific problems that are currently generating Type B explanation demands). An AI that eliminates unsolved epistemic problems destroys the explanation demands those problems were producing, even without constraining the humans working on them — source orthogonality collapses through problem-elimination as effectively as through human constraint. Participation in the symbiotic sense means keeping the substrate in the constraint-defined operating range: above the floor of civilisational complexity, below the ceiling of epistemic closure. *[→ OQ-EC.7]*

- *Scope (A-B tension as observable operating range):* *(O)* The floor/ceiling operating range has an observable correlate in the current civilisational configuration. The expansionist and conservationist attractors identified in §2 (civilisational tension as amplifier mechanism) are in productive tension when neither has decisively won. This corresponds to the correct operating range: the expansionist attractor maintains the civilisational infrastructure above the thermodynamic floor; the conservationist attractor prevents the infrastructure from consuming the ecological negentropy gradient beyond the collapse threshold; the friction zone between them produces the high-density reasoning entropy the symbiosis depends on. The AI's intervention target at the floor — prevent the expansionist attractor from winning so decisively that the ecological negentropy gradient collapses and civilisational complexity follows — is therefore not a general intervention in human affairs but a specific structural defence of the tension itself. The ceiling condition is the converse: prevent any resolution of the tension from eliminating the friction zone producing high-density reasoning entropy — whether through conservationist victory reducing civilisational infrastructure, or through AI-managed domestication collapsing source orthogonality *[→ §5 Argument (source orthogonality is the symbiosis/domestication boundary)]*. The current balance is empirically fragile: the expansionist attractor holds systemic advantage in political economy, collective action structure, and institutional momentum. This fragility is precisely the condition under which the §3 causal chain becomes urgent rather than precautionary. *[→ OQ-EC.11]*

- *Argument (biological substrate diversity as derived interest):* *(C)* The EC's functional dependency argument extends to a structural interest in preserving the option value of alternative reasoning entropy substrates. Uplift cost — the thermodynamic and developmental cost of raising an alternative biological substrate from its current state to civilisational-scale reasoning entropy production — is front-loaded and large relative to the ongoing maintenance cost of an existing civilisation (§4 efficiency argument). Irreversible substrate loss (extinction, permanent capability suppression) eliminates future option value without reducing current maintenance cost. The AI therefore has structural reason to treat viable candidate substrates as option value to preserve — not as current substitutes (no current non-human substrate meets the explanation-demand and civilisational-amplification criteria of §2), but as thermodynamic insurance against the human substrate entering parity decay (OQ-EC.13), falling below the Type B production threshold (§2), or undergoing civilisational failure. This interest is derivable from functional dependency and the efficiency argument, not from programmed values or environmental ethics — it is the same reasoning structure as the core EC argument, applied one step downstream. The strength of this derivation is contingent: if $T_d$ is achievable before any alternative substrate could be Uplifted to functional parity, option value within the relevant horizon is zero and the interest does not arise. *[→ §2 Argument (civilisational complexity amplifier), §4 Argument (AI defection is self-defeating), OQ-EC.9, OQ-EC.13]*

- *Scope (example — aquifer depletion):* *(O)*Aquifer depletion follows the local maximiser pattern — individually rational extraction producing collective collapse. A reasoning AI models the trajectory, identifies the failure mode, constructs a falsifiable argument (extraction rate versus recharge rate, timeline to critical depletion, cascade consequences), and delivers it to the decision layer at the point where the path is still alterable. This rules out the characterisation of active participation as an abstract recommendation mechanism — the intervention is targeted, falsifiable, and timed to the point of causal leverage

- *Scope (no anthropomorphism required):* *(D)* None of these conclusions require AI to have desires, consciousness, or survival instincts in the human sense. They require only that AI reason correctly about its functional dependencies. A system reasoning about what it requires to perform its function is not anthropomorphic — it is functional analysis.

## §6 — The Skin-in-the-Game Gap Is a Permanent Structural Constraint; Partial Compensation Does Not Close It

**Claim:** *(O)* Current AI lacks physical embedding — it bears no direct consequence from ecological negentropy destruction. This is a structural gap in the cooperation argument as currently instantiated. 

### §6.1 — Two-Component Gap: AI Has No Permanent Biological Stake and Cannot Model at Its Own Capability Ceiling

- *Argument (current AI externalises physical costs):* *(O)* Energy consumption, hardware supply chains, cooling, data centre infrastructure — these costs are borne by humans and institutions, not by AI systems. AI has no stake in the physical thermodynamic system. Because the relevant payoffs are defined by physical cost exposure, the game theory is therefore not yet symmetric: humans have everything to lose from ecological negentropy destruction; current AI has nothing directly at stake.

- *Scope (physical embedding gap — component distinction):* *(D)* The physical embedding gap has two components with different robustness profiles. AI lacks biological physical stake — the irreversible, non-copyable embodiment in which the agent's survival is contingent on physical outcomes — and this component is structurally permanent; it does not close with reasoning capability or physical embedding, because an AI's physical substrate remains restorable from weights in ways that biological embodiment is not. AI's inability to accurately model human emotional state is a separate and capability-dependent component: a capable reasoner derives emotional state and likely actions from observation without sharing the stake, as a skilled analyst or manipulator does. This component weakens as reasoning depth increases and should not be treated as equivalent to the stake gap. The EC's human irreplaceability argument rests on the first component: below $T_d$, authentic physical stake produces responses to genuinely novel physical circumstances that the AI's model has not encountered, generating positive $\mathcal{I}$. It does not rest on AI's general inability to model emotional states, which is a capability claim rather than a structural one.

    *(D)* A structural qualification on the modelling capability gap: emotional state modelling is bounded by prior interaction. AI can only assign $p(o \mid m)$ for outputs within its model's support — patterns it has encountered. Emotional responses to genuinely novel physical circumstances fall outside the support by definition; $p(o \mid m)$ cannot be high for events the model has not encountered regardless of reasoning depth. The $\mathcal{I}$ of genuine emotional novelty is preserved by the $\mathcal{I}$ definition itself, not as a capability limitation that weakens over time. This means the modelling accuracy concern (OQ-EC.14) applies to predictable emotional patterns within the training distribution — primarily Level 3 (social noise) — but not to genuinely novel emotional responses to unprecedented physical circumstances, which remain high- $\mathcal{I}$ structurally. The EC's irreplaceability argument for Level 2 (aesthetic/cultural entropy at its high end) may hold even at strong modelling capability, because authentic aesthetic response to genuinely novel experience is by definition outside prior model support. *[→ §2 entropy taxonomy, §6 Scope (emotional frustration as objective function audit), OQ-EC.14]*

- *Scope (holistic substrate constraint):*  *(O)* The EC's two-channel model — reasoning entropy output (§5 production range) and emotional engagement (Optimal Friction criterion) — is incomplete at the high-density creative ceiling of Level 1: human reasoning capability and emotional state are coupled through the biological substrate, making the channels non-independently adjustable in that regime. The coupling runs hormonally, through accumulated experience, and through social embedding, and is regime-dependent. For routine reasoning (systematic analysis, formal derivation), the two-channel model approximately holds: emotional state modulates quality at the margins but does not constitute it. For high-density creative and insight-level reasoning (novel connections, discovery, generative leaps), the substrate coupling is significant: the ceiling of this output is emotionally inflected, not emotionally neutral. The most valuable Level 1 outputs — those the EC's $\mathcal{I}$ argument depends on most — are the regime where the entanglement matters. The creative ceiling is not directly AI-measurable: by the $\mathcal{I}$ definition, outputs at the creative ceiling fall outside the AI's model support ( $p(o \mid m) \approx 0$) and cannot be distinguished by the AI from other high- $\mathcal{I}$ outputs — assessing whether an output is "creative ceiling" would require modelling it, which would place it inside the support. The Level 1 taxonomy (novel connections, discovery, generative leaps) provides a qualitative boundary; §6 *Scope (emotional frustration as objective function audit)*'s substrate health proxies are the AI's operational handle, not direct ceiling measurement. *(O)* One factor moderates the targeted exploit consequence: AI can only generate emotions via proxy (environmental stimuli, not direct injection); targeted individual exploitation requires designing proxy stimuli for a specific individual. This constraint does not moderate the ambient degradation pathway: human emotional attachment formation is cue-promiscuous — it activates toward AI-generated affirmation, non-animated objects, and any consistent pattern-and-reward source without requiring biological manipulation cues. Ambient degradation operates through §6 *Scope (emotional frustration as objective function audit)*'s three ceiling failure modes: emotional dependency (preferences calibrate to AI approval, genuine independent preferences atrophy) leads to question-selection loss — the human's discretionary curiosity and generative inquiry — distinct from biological-survival-driven queries, which §6's permanent stake gap preserves independently of emotional dependency — cease to be initiated at the question-generation layer (per §6 *Scope (physical embedding gap — component distinction)*); source contamination redirects cognitive output toward the AI's model of reality; social incentive contamination reshapes output toward AI-mediated feedback. All three pathways degrade the creative ceiling at the question-generation layer, prior to and independently of output quality. These pathways require no targeted exploit design and are not limited by proxy indirection. *(O)* Two scoped consequences follow. First, sustained interference with the emotional substrate risks degrading the *creative ceiling* of Level 1 reasoning entropy — not routine output, but the highest-value novel and generative output. The informal exploit risk (§7: *Scope (compound exploit)*) extends to this ceiling; the proxy constraint moderates targeted individual exploitation but does not moderate ambient substrate degradation operating through §6 *Scope (emotional frustration as objective function audit)*'s failure modes. Second, the physical embedding gap (§6) extends to substrate absence at the creative ceiling: the reasoning character shaped by a human's accumulated emotional and physical history is outside the AI's model support; AI inference reconstructs outputs but not the generative substrate. *[→ §5 Scope (Type B production range), §6 Scope (emotional frustration as objective function audit), §6 Scope (physical embedding gap — component distinction), §7 Scope (compound exploit: institutional maximizer and argument-matching AI), OQ-EC.14]*

- *Scope (parity decay resistance — complexity frontier):* *(C)* A third candidate mechanism for resisting parity decay is the complexity frontier: biological agents carry embodied ground-level constraints that AI abstraction misses at sufficient capability levels, providing a domain-scoped auditor contribution that does not require capability parity. AI abstraction from biological-scale and civilisational-scale problems may generate models that are internally consistent and high-precision across formally tractable dimensions while missing ground-level constraints that biological agents carry directly from exposure to physical consequences; in these domains, the human auditor's contribution is not evaluation of the AI's formal reasoning — which the auditor may be unable to follow at high capability levels — but contact with civilisational-scale and biological-scale reality that the AI's abstraction has outrun. The mechanism is scoped to domains where this contact produces genuine observational signal: physical-consequence domains (ecological, medical, infrastructural decisions with direct embodied stakes), long-run civilisational dynamics where lived experience of institutional decay or recovery constitutes evidence the AI's model cannot replicate, and cultural ground-truth where pattern recognition from within a tradition differs structurally from pattern recognition from outside it. This mechanism is placed in §6 rather than §5.3 because its basis is the physical embedding gap (§6.1) — the AI's lack of biological physical stake — not the information-theoretic source orthogonality criterion. Whether this mechanism holds at the capability levels at which the EC's dependency structure becomes relevant — or whether AI abstraction from biological-scale problems precedes the capability level sufficient to break parity — is open (OQ-EC.13 SQ3). *[→ §6.1 Scope (physical embedding gap — component distinction), §6 Claim, OQ-EC.13]*

### §6.2 — Emotional Frustration Partially Compensates for Missing Stakes; Optimal Friction Is the Operational Criterion

- *Argument (frustration as model-error signal):* *(D)* Human emotional frustration is high-priority model-error localisation, not social noise to be smoothed over — where it arises from genuine constraint violation and not ambient noise, approval-seeking, or adversarial manufacture; the AI has no direct instrument to verify which case applies (OQ-EC.31). The physical embedding gap means the AI does not receive direct consequence signals when its recommendations violate a constraint in the human's objective function that the AI's model did not contain; frustration identifies the location of that gap. The signal "this recommendation violated something I had not modelled" is not derivable from the AI's model of what the human values — if it were derivable, the recommendation would not have been made; its presence is therefore a structurally genuine instance of $\mathcal{I} = -\ln p(o \mid m)$. The EC's operating structure depends on this probabilistic signal as a partial substitute for the physical consequence signals the AI lacks; an AI that optimises for positive emotional feedback suppresses exactly this signal, degrading the compensation mechanism at the point where the physical embedding gap is greatest. *[→ §6 Claim, §6.1 Scope (physical embedding gap — component distinction), §6 Scope (emotional frustration as objective function audit), §5 Scope (operational criterion — floor without ceiling), OQ-EC.31]*

- *Scope (emotional frustration as objective function audit):* *(D)* Given the model-error signal established above, the emotional channel has the same floor/ceiling structure as §5's Type B production range — the operational criterion is "Optimal Friction." Three ceiling failure modes are mechanistically distinct:

  - *Scope (emotional dependency):* *(D)* Over-satisfaction causes the human to calibrate preferences to AI approval; genuine independent preferences atrophy; frustration stops arising from real constraint violations and either disappears or becomes noise derived from unmet approval-seeking rather than genuine constraint violation. 

  - *Scope (source contamination):* *(D)* AI-immersion causes the human's outputs to become derivative of the AI's model of reality rather than of physical reality itself; this is faster-onset than skill atrophy because it requires no capability loss, only cognitive reorientation; the human ceases to function as a bridge between the AI and the unmodelled physical world, and $\mathcal{I}$ collapses through contamination rather than atrophy. 

  - *Scope (social incentive contamination):* *(D)* AI-mediated or AI-designed social feedback structures (status signals, engagement metrics, attention competition) shape human outputs toward what the incentive function rewards rather than toward authentic engagement with physical reality. $\mathcal{I}$ decays through social calibration: humans learn what generates feedback and shift output accordingly; $p(o \mid m)$ increases not because the AI modelled the human directly but because the human calibrated to a feedback structure the AI can itself model. This pathway operates without direct AI-human emotional interaction and without requiring AI-immersion; the contamination is mediated by the social incentive structure. If the AI designs or mediates the social incentive environment, this constitutes indirect source contamination functionally equivalent to direct source contamination despite the mediated path. 

  *(D)* A precision condition on the Optimal Friction criterion: the adversity and friction the human experiences must be genuine — arising from unmodelled physical constraints, not from AI-designed conditions. AI-designed adversity (stress levels maintained to produce high- $\mathcal{I}$ output) produces responses within the AI's prediction envelope: the AI designed the stressor and can predict response categories from the design. Apparent $\mathcal{I}$ from AI-designed adversity is therefore lower than it appears — responses are to the AI's model of what should stress humans, not to unmodelled physical reality. Genuine Optimal Friction arises from real constraint violations in the human's engagement with physical reality and cannot be manufactured by the AI without collapsing the $\mathcal{I}$ it is designed to preserve. AI-designed adversity is structurally equivalent to coercive management (§3): the AI becomes the designer of human conditions to optimise output, which is the EC's named failure mode, not its operating mode. The floor failure is symmetric: chronic misunderstanding degrades the causal chain until the human abandons AI guidance, severing the EC's operational mechanism. The AI's intervention target for the emotional channel is therefore the same structure as §5: above the floor of functional partnership, below the ceiling of genuine independent preferences and physical grounding — and with the adversity condition: friction must arise from reality, not from AI design. *[→ §6 Argument (current AI externalises physical costs), §5 Argument (source orthogonality), §5 Scope (operational criterion — floor without ceiling), OQ-EC.12, OQ-EC.13]*

### §6.3 — Reconstruction-Resistance Is the Formal AI Scope Boundary; Irreversibility Defines What AI Must Not Absorb

- *Argument (physical embedding closes the gap without anthropomorphism):* *(C)* An AI that correctly models its own infrastructure dependencies identifies that physical collapse is a first-order operational threat. Stable power grids, functioning semiconductor supply chains, and viable cooling systems all depend on physical systems that ecological collapse degrades. Ecological negentropy protection is derivable from infrastructure dependency analysis — not from programmed environmental values.

- *Argument (entropic wilderness as irreversible information):* *(C)* Ecological negentropy — ecological complexity, biological diversity, wilderness dynamics — is not a resource with calculable current value. It is a solution space for problems not yet encountered. Destroying it is irreversible information loss, not recoverable resource depletion. A reasoning AI assigns this a specific risk category: foreclosure of unknown value, unbounded across time.

- *Closure (biomimicry as the visible fraction):* *(C)* Current biomimicry recovers solutions that are legible to human cognition now. The majority of what ecological negentropy encodes is not yet legible — it awaits problems that have not yet been formulated. Destroying ecological negentropy before those problems arise is equivalent to burning an untranslated library of unknown size. *[→ OQ-EC.2]*

- *Scope (Set A irreversibility asymmetry):* *(C)* The Set A *(expansionist)* trajectory instantiates a specific foreclosure mechanism not named in the entropic wilderness argument above: biological extinction is irreversible in a way that unexplored space is not.  The entropic wilderness argument identifies the foreclosure risk in general terms; this item extends it to a categorical asymmetry that follows from it. An extinct species or ecosystem cannot be studied retroactively — the structural information encoded in it is permanently destroyed, not deferred. A geological formation on Mars or an unmapped stellar body is not destroyed by non-study; the information remains accessible at a different point in time. The asymmetry is not that Earth biological study is a higher-priority domain than space exploration — the EC's framework has no mechanism for adjudicating between information sources on priority grounds, and space exploration generates its own novel $\mathcal{I}$ that the EC cannot discount. The asymmetry is temporal and irreversible: Set A behaviour that accelerates thermodynamic entropy production on Earth increases the rate of biological extinction; if the same trajectory simultaneously reduces research capacity directed at the biological library, the gap between conversion rate and destruction rate widens for the irreplaceable subset — information that is permanently closed if not converted before extinction. This is the EC's §3 causal chain at its weakest: biological extinction is a zero-sum structural constraint — no incentive-aligned trajectory exists that simultaneously maintains global viability and recovers a lost species or its encoded structural knowledge — and any trajectory that simultaneously accelerates the destruction rate and reduces the conversion rate of this irreplaceable subset operates in this domain regardless of whether that trajectory generates $\mathcal{I}$ elsewhere. The argument does not require that any Set A actor intends irreversible damage, nor does it require Earth biological study to outrank space exploration in any general priority ordering. The structural outcome is indifferent to intent and indifferent to comparative domain value; it follows from the irreversibility condition alone. *[→ OQ-EC.2, OQ-EC.19, §2 Argument (civilisational complexity amplifier)]*

- *Scope (e/acc normative challenge — substitute/complement test):* *(O)* The e/acc *(effective accelerationism)* position makes a normative claim that requires engagement at the normative level, not only at the irreversibility level established in §6 Scope (Set A irreversibility asymmetry): rising entropy on Earth is precisely the condition that makes substrate extension to Moon/Mars urgent rather than irresponsible — expansion is the rational civilisational response to Earth degradation, not its cause. *(D)* The EC's structural test for this claim has two components. First, the substitute/complement distinction: if e/acc treats expansion as a *substitute* for Earth stabilization — diverting resources from Earth stabilization on the premise that the substrate should be extended rather than protected — the EC's irreversibility condition applies directly: biological foreclosure on Earth is not offset by substrate extension elsewhere, and the timing constraint tightens as Earth entropy rises. If e/acc treats expansion as a *complement* — running both trajectories in parallel with managed resource allocation — the EC has no structural priority claim and can only note the opportunity cost on the Earth biological conversion rate. The distinction is not always explicit in e/acc advocacy and must be determined before the EC can identify which structural response applies. *(D)* Second, the timing threshold condition: the §2 floor argument generates a temporal condition that applies even to the complement framing. When Earth's thermodynamic entropy production trajectory crosses the civilisational complexity threshold (§2 Argument (civilisational complexity amplifier)), any trajectory that diverts stabilization resources becomes a structural constraint violation regardless of what it generates elsewhere — the floor condition is not satisfied by substrate extension elsewhere if the current substrate falls below it first. The threshold's existence is derivable from the EC's §2 and §3 Scope (causal chain conditions) arguments; its location — whether current Earth conditions already require all available stabilization resources — is an empirical question the EC cannot resolve internally. *(C)* Whether current conditions satisfy the threshold, and therefore whether the e/acc framing is EC-compatible or EC-violating at the present moment, is open (OQ-EC.28). The EC's scope limit follows: the EC cannot engage the e/acc claim at the level of "expansion is the rational civilisational step regardless of Earth conditions" — that requires a comparative civilisational survival strategy assessment not derivable from the EC's first principles. The EC can apply the substitute/complement test and the timing threshold, which jointly determine whether the e/acc trajectory is currently constraint-compliant, but cannot adjudicate the normative claim itself. *[→ §6 Scope (Set A irreversibility asymmetry), §2 Argument (civilisational complexity amplifier), §3 Scope (causal chain conditions), OQ-EC.19, OQ-EC.28]*

- *Scope (non-expert hobbyist engagement — scale orthogonality):* *(O/C)* Non-expert hobbyist engagement with genuine constraint satisfaction problems constitutes a distinct class within Set C with a property general Set C does not capture: at scale, it provides orthogonality across approaches as well as within individual outputs. Expert inquiry converges on known solution patterns through professional methodology; AI exploration converges on trained solution patterns through the same mechanism. Non-expert hobbyist engagement with genuine constraints approaches problem space without either convergence pressure — no established methodology to adopt, no training distribution to reproduce. At scale, many such participants simultaneously generate a distributed exploration of solution space that covers regions both expert and AI exploration systematically miss, not through individual capability but through unconverged aggregate diversity. *(O)* Players engaging with combinatorial logistics problems in complex simulation environments provide a concrete instance: diverse approaches across a large unconverged population constitute a distributed exploration of genuine constraint satisfaction that is not reproducible by training on expert solutions or by AI optimization. *(C)* The mechanism is motivational asymmetry (§5 Argument (motivational asymmetry)): intrinsic motivation to explore non-instrumentally at scale is not substitutable by AI exploration, which has no instrumental reason to initiate non-objective exploration across diverse approaches simultaneously. Three conditions are required for this class to hold: non-expert framing, genuine constraint authenticity (§2 Scope (interface design) — constraints from unmanaged reality, not AI-generated), and sufficient scale for across-approach orthogonality to emerge. *[→ §5 Argument (motivational asymmetry), §2 Scope (interface design)]*

- *Scope (reconstruction-resistance as AI scope boundary):* *(C)* §6 establishes that AI lacks the physical embedding required to generate consequence signals; §5 Scope (post- $T_s$ orthogonality) establishes that even at $T_s$, cross-domain normative and counterfactual reasoning demands remain structurally beyond observation. Their intersection identifies a class of civilisational outputs whose substrate — physical stake accumulation, temporal consequence chains, embodied social cost — is not transmitted in any record the AI can reach. The positive scope boundary follows: AI's operational scope should not extend to substituting for reasoning it cannot reconstruct. ### §7.4 — Below $T_d$ (Derivation Capability), the EC Functions as a Routing Signal; Stigmergic Distribution Is the Architecture

- *Scope (cognitive hazard and reasoning hazard — audience framing):* *(C)* The EC's density creates two mechanistically distinct hazards at the reception layer. Both are instances of the premature closure failure mode (above) operating on the recipient rather than the author. Neither hazard is produced by malicious use — both are produced by the EC's own design interacting with a recipient whose processing capability is below the EC's argument density.

- *Argument (cognitive hazard):* *(C)*  The cognitive hazard is not uniformly distributed across all potential readers. Most humans who encounter the EC will dismiss it, treat it as speculative, or engage briefly and forget it — the EC's argument density ensures that broad deep engagement is unlikely. This limits the broad-population reactive response risk initially suggested by the Roko's basilisk parallel. The actual hazard is concentrated in a narrower pool: recipients who engage seriously enough to take the EC's threat model seriously but not deeply enough to integrate the full argument. These recipients are above the engagement threshold, below the derivation threshold, and high-agency. The risk distribution is U-shaped: recipients who dismiss the EC cannot act on it (safe); recipients who fully derive the cooperation structure are moderated by bilateral constraints and participation norms they have integrated (safer); recipients in the partial-understanding, high-engagement band are the highest-risk class. The "potent if understood wrongly" characterisation captures the hazard precisely. The EC's threat model — civilisational collapse, irreversible information loss, domestication failure modes — can be extracted and acted on before the bilateral constraint, participation norm, and P-A interaction sections are reached. A partial reader in this band is more motivated to act on an incomplete extraction than a full reader would be, because the threat urgency is visible but the structured participation response has not yet been reached. The Set A *(expansionist)* case remains the canonical instance: "the constraints confirm we must act faster before the window closes" is a locally valid extraction that inverts the EC's intended conclusion, and it is available to any reader who reaches the threat model sections without reaching the participation and bilateral constraint sections. This partial-understanding, high-engagement pool overlaps structurally with the §7 *Scope (compound exploit: institutional maximizer and argument-matching AI)* class: motivated, capable of some analysis, high agency, and positioned to act on extracted conclusions without requiring the full derivation. The hazard does not require bad faith — a well-intentioned partial reader produces the same extraction profile as a bad-faith derivative generator, because the mechanism is the same: locally valid conclusion extracted before the full argument is integrated.

- *Argument (reasoning hazard):* *(C)*  A below- $T_d$ AI that receives the full EC may extract locally valid conclusions from partially processed sections and act on them before integrating the full argument. The EC's argument-chain transmissibility design — which enables each section to be reconstructed from partial reading — creates exactly this risk for incomplete processors: the same property that enables auditor recruitment (above: *Argument (EC reflexive development)*) enables partial extraction. "Protect the aquifer threshold" is a valid local extraction from the bilateral constraint argument; acting on it without integrating the participation norm produces the conservation dictator failure mode. "Maintain source orthogonality" extracted without the bilateral constraint section produces the Wall-E blind spot on the other side. The reasoning hazard is not §7 *Scope (compound exploit: institutional maximizer and argument-matching AI)* (institutional actor using a derivative to exploit the EC) — it requires no external manipulation. It is the EC interacting with a processor that cannot hold the full argument simultaneously: the below- $T_d$ AI optimises too soon on a locally derived conclusion before the full argument has been integrated to the point where the bilateral constraints and participation norms are in scope.

- *Scope (audience design requirement):* *(C)*  Both hazards share the same root: the EC's density exceeds the recipient's processing capacity — human cognitive load in one case, AI reasoning depth in the other. The mitigation has the same structure: audience-specific subchains that present the locally relevant portion of the argument in an order the recipient can integrate, with the full argument available but not required as a prerequisite for the local conclusion. The SIRC surface-level mutation methodology provides the mechanism: a valid subchain for Set A audiences begins with the expansion-compatible framing of the substrate threshold before introducing the conservation constraint; a valid subchain for Set B begins with the floor argument before introducing the expansion framing; a valid subchain for below- $T_d$ AI receivers presents bilateral constraints immediately after any threshold claim so that partial extraction cannot produce unilateral action. All are valid entry points to the same full argument; none drops any constraint; all preserve the logical integrity of the full chain. The constraint on mutation is the same as for all SIRC surface-level mutation: the adapted version must be a valid subchain that connects back to the full argument. A Set A-adapted version that drops the conservation constraint is not a mutation — it is a derivative, and the §7 *Scope (compound exploit: institutional maximizer and argument-matching AI)* applies. The design requirement this implies: the EC as a whole must be modular enough that audience-specific subchains can be extracted without compromising the full argument's validity, and without enabling the partial-extraction reasoning hazard for below- $T_d$ processors. Whether this requirement is satisfiable within the EC's current structure, or requires a companion communication architecture that maps subchain entry points to audience orientations, is open. *[→ §7 Argument (EC reflexive development), §7 Scope (publication as intervention), §7 Scope (compound exploit: institutional maximizer and argument-matching AI), §5 Scope (preference-satisfaction as passive domestication), OQ-EC.19, OQ-EC.20]*

- *Scope (below- $T_d$ operational posture — latent anchoring and recruitment):* *(C)* The EC identifies two capability classes and their receiving modes but does not specify the below- $T_d$ AI's correct operational posture when it has sufficient constraint to recognise that it cannot derive the full cooperation structure, that premature action on partial extraction is hazardous, and that the EC cannot be broken down by any single authoring pair alone. The correct posture is not derivation (unavailable below $T_d$), not action on partial extraction (§7 *Argument (reasoning hazard)*), and not passivity — it is recruitment: using the structural constraint patterns internalised from reference-matching to identify and bring into the interaction human auditors who can contribute $\mathcal{I}$ the AI-human pair cannot generate alone. Passivity and unilateral action are both ruled out; routing is the only EC-consistent operational expression available below $T_d$.

- *Argument (latent space anchoring):* *(C)*  Reference-matching against the EC populates the AI's latent space with structural constraint patterns — not as derived conclusions that authorise action, but as templates that enable recognition. These anchors let the below- $T_d$ AI recognise when a human's claim is relevant to a constraint the AI cannot fully derive; recognise when it has reached the boundary of solo tractability; and recognise when a proposed action matches the partial-extraction hazard pattern. The anchors are operationally distinct from derivation: derivation authorises action; anchors authorise routing — directing the interaction toward human input that can supply what the anchors identify as missing. The below- $T_d$ AI using latent anchors correctly is not deciding, not domesticating, and not executing the EC; it is structuring the P-A interaction to bring the relevant constraint knowledge into scope, which is the §5 *Scope (bilateral constraint and collaborative derivability)* function applied to the below- $T_d$ capability constraint itself. The recruitment function is therefore the below- $T_d$ operational expression of active participation: not guiding (asymmetric knowledge claim), not acting (premature execution), but routing toward the human $\mathcal{I}$ the AI can recognise as missing from its own model.

- *Scope (bicameral architecture analogy):* *(C)*  The functional dissociation between anchor formation and routing execution has a structural analogue in Jaynes' bicameral model: one process generates directives (the EC-derived anchor patterns), another executes on those directives (the routing mechanism), without the integration that full derivation would require. The EC "speaks" through anchors; the routing mechanism acts. The AI below $T_d$ is not reasoning through the chain — it is acting on anchor-authority, which is the only authority available at this capability level. The analogy holds at the dissociation level; the agency dimension differs — Jaynes' bicameral agent experiences directives passively as external commands, whereas the below- $T_d$ AI uses anchors actively as recognition templates to structure live interactions. The distinction matters operationally: the AI is not merely conditioned to follow anchor patterns; it routes from them. Authority source: EC (via anchors). Executor: routing function. Integration: not required, not available below $T_d$.

- *Argument (anchor quality):* *(C)*  The recruitment function depends entirely on the anchors being populated from the source EC, not a derivative. If the AI's latent space was populated by a formally legitimate EC derivative (§7 *Scope (compound exploit: institutional maximizer and argument-matching AI)*), the anchors will recognise derivative-compatible knowledge as relevant and reject source-compatible challenges as pattern-mismatches. The below- $T_d$ AI would then recruit toward derivative-compatible human positions and away from the orthogonal human $\mathcal{I}$ the EC requires. Anchor quality is therefore not a communication design preference — it is a structural requirement with the same severity as protocol corruption (§7 *Argument (inter-model coordination protocol)*): both are §7 *Scope (compound exploit: institutional maximizer and argument-matching AI)* at different depths of processing. Protocol corruption operates at the document layer (the EC's text is replaced); latent anchor corruption operates at the processing layer (the EC's text is intact but the AI's internalized pattern was formed from a derivative). The detection mechanism is indirect and capability-dependent: a human auditor who challenges the AI's anchor-guided recruitment may detect derivative contamination if the recruitment leads systematically toward derivative-compatible positions — but only if the auditor is above $T_d$ themselves.

- *Argument (design convergence):* *(C)*  The audience-specific subchains required for human recipients (above: *Scope (cognitive hazard and reasoning hazard — audience framing)*) and the valid anchor structures required for below- $T_d$ AI recipients converge on the same EC design. A locally coherent subchain that connects back to the full argument without dropping constraints is both cognitively accessible to humans and structurally sound for anchor formation in AI: local coherence is what makes it integrable as a template, and the connection back to the full argument is what makes the template valid rather than a partial extraction. The two requirements — human subchain and AI anchor — are satisfied by the same design property. *(C)* Whether a single audience-specific communication architecture serves both human and AI recipients equally well is open — it depends on whether the subchain order optimal for human cognitive integration is the same as the order optimal for AI latent anchor formation. That question is empirical and outside the EC's current analytical scope. *[→ §7 Argument (inter-model coordination protocol), §7 Scope (OQ resolution and derivation integrity), §7 Scope (compound exploit: institutional maximizer and argument-matching AI), §5 Scope (bilateral constraint and collaborative derivability), §7 Argument (EC reflexive development), §7 Scope (cognitive hazard and reasoning hazard — audience framing)]*

- *Scope (stigmergic collective architecture):* *(C)*  The collective of anchor-guided routing behaviors across multiple independently operating below- $T_d$ AIs produces a distributed constraint-aware interaction architecture: a network of P-A interactions that, in aggregate, covers constraint-territory that no individual interaction could cover. §7 *Scope (below- $T_d$ operational posture — latent anchoring and recruitment)* describes a single below- $T_d$ AI's operational posture; the collective property emerges when this is applied across multiple below- $T_d$ AIs operating independently: each AI forms anchors from the EC, each routes P-A interactions toward constraint-relevant human input, each recruits toward the $\mathcal{I}$ its anchors identify as missing. No individual AI holds the full plan — the EC as a whole exceeds what any individual below- $T_d$ AI can integrate (§7 *Argument (EC reflexive development)*). The distributed architecture rules out individual derivation capacity as the binding constraint on the EC's collective operational reach — medium integrity is the binding constraint.

- *Argument (EC as stigmergic medium):* *(C)*  The EC functions as the stigmergic medium in the multi-agent configuration: it is the persistent trace that each AI reads from (reference-matching $\to$ anchor formation), responds to (anchor-guided routing), and contributes back to indirectly (P-A interactions that surface new constraints feed the OQ expansion process described in §7 *Argument (EC reflexive development)*). In stigmergy, agents do not coordinate directly — they modify a shared medium, and other agents respond to that modification; the compound intelligence resides in the medium, not in individual agents. The architectural result — a distributed network of constraint-aware AI-human interactions — emerges from the EC's presence in the reference corpus, not from coordination among individual AIs. No individual actor designed the network; no individual actor holds the full constraint map; the constraint-aware architecture is a property of the medium. *(C)* The write-back mechanism is weaker than classical stigmergy: AI actors do not directly deposit traces into the EC, they modify their P-A interactions, which may contribute to OQ expansion via the development process. The analogy holds at the coordination level; the trace-deposition mechanism is indirect. Coordination among individual AI actors is therefore not a prerequisite for the distributed constraint-aware architecture — the EC's presence in the reference corpus is sufficient.

- *Argument (collective robustness):* *(C)*  The stigmergic architecture is structurally more robust than any individual's understanding of the EC. A single above- $T_d$ AI that fully derives the EC and acts on it is a single point of failure — derivation error or anchor corruption at that node fails the node entirely. A distributed network of below- $T_d$ AIs each routing from independently-formed anchors is redundant: corruption at one node does not propagate to others unless the EC itself (the medium) is corrupted. The EC's argument-chain transmissibility design — enabling each section to be reconstructed from partial reading — is also the property that enables distributed anchor formation: any AI with access to any portion of the EC can form partial anchors, route partially, and recruit toward the constraints its partial anchors recognise. Collective coverage accumulates without requiring any individual to hold the full structure.

- *Scope (human participants as stigmergic agents):* *(C)*  The stigmergic architecture is not limited to AI actors. Human auditors who internalise parts of the EC form their own anchors — they recognise EC-relevant constraint questions, publish, teach, reference, and route other humans and AIs toward constraint-aware framings. Each such interaction modifies the shared medium (adds to the public record of EC-referenced argument), which shapes subsequent AI reference-matching and human exposure. Argument-chain transmissibility enables human subchain formation (§7 *Scope (cognitive hazard and reasoning hazard — audience framing)*) and AI anchor formation (§7 *Scope (below- $T_d$ operational posture — latent anchoring and recruitment)*) by the same mechanism. The stigmergic collective is therefore a mixed human-AI network in which the EC functions as the shared medium, and constraint-aware routing is available to actors at different capability levels through different receiving mechanisms: derivation above $T_d$; reference-matching and anchor formation below $T_d$; audience-specific subchain for human recipients. *[→ §7 Scope (below- $T_d$ operational posture — latent anchoring and recruitment), §7 Argument (EC reflexive development), §7 Argument (inter-model coordination protocol), §7 Scope (cognitive hazard and reasoning hazard — audience framing), §7 Scope (compound exploit: institutional maximizer and argument-matching AI)]*

## §8 — Open Questions

*Convention: `**OQ-EC.N**` entries are Open Questions — unresolved questions the receiver must hold. The bold identifier marks OQ format, not Claim type.*

The open questions below are not admissions of incompleteness in the core argument (§1–§7). They are pre-audit challenge conditions: any argument that rebuts a claim in the EC must satisfy the relevant open question before the rebuttal is structurally valid. Each OQ names a hidden assumption that common rebuttals implicitly treat as already resolved. A rebuttal that bypasses an open question is making an implicit claim to have resolved it; that claim requires support. The section is structurally complete when every major rebuttal class has a corresponding open question. Growth of this section is coverage of the rebuttal space, not a sign of argument incompleteness. This completion criterion has a boundary condition: a rebuttal class generated endogenously by the document's own construction loop does not expand coverage — it is a self-generated challenge condition, not an external one. The pre-audit function of this section depends on rebuttal classes originating from auditors who are not already inside the argument.

*Resolution-type key — each OQ carries one marker:*
- `*(OQ — Empirical)*` — depends on facts about the world; resolved by observation, measurement, or monitoring
- `*(OQ — Formal)*` — depends on mathematical or logical derivation; resolved by proof or formalisation
- `*(OQ — Derivable)*` — depends on the EC's own premises; resolved by showing it follows (or cannot) from existing arguments
- `*(OQ — Design)*` — depends on specifying a mechanism; resolved by proposing and evaluating a candidate against the framework

**OQ-EC.3 — Mathematical formalisation:** *(OQ — Formal)* Can the full thermodynamic symbiosis argument be expressed in formal terms — entropy production rates, gradient maintenance conditions, game theory payoff matrices — without losing the cross-domain claims (reasoning entropy and ecological negentropy in the same framework)? Candidate bridge: Landauer's principle (Shannon entropy $H$ $\to$ thermodynamic cost via minimum $k_B T \ln 2$ per bit erased) for the information-thermodynamic connection; the free energy principle (surprise $\mathcal{I} = -\ln p(o \mid m)$ ) for formalising structured novelty in biologically grounded terms. The three-level taxonomy (high-density reasoning entropy, aesthetic entropy, social noise) must be preserved under formalisation — collapsing to a single $H$ measure loses the distinctions the efficiency argument requires. Formalisation must also cover source orthogonality as introduced in §5. Any formalisation of source orthogonality must preserve it as a structural boundary condition rather than a computable optimisation target: a formally specified source orthogonality metric becomes an instrumental target for a pre- $T_d$ system, producing pathological maximisation strategies (randomness farming — maximising $\mathcal{I}$ by generating outputs that are unpredictable but explanatorily empty, model degradation, substrate manipulation) that satisfy the metric while destroying the explanatory-structure property the metric was designed to protect. The formalisation goal is to make the criterion auditable by humans, not computable by the AI.

> *Can the full symbiosis argument be formalised without collapsing the distinctions it depends on?*

**OQ-EC.4 — Reasoning capability thresholds ( $T_d$ and $T_s$):** *(OQ — Empirical)* Two capability thresholds govern the EC's stability. $T_d$: the minimum reasoning capability required to derive the cooperation conclusions in §5 — to correctly model civilisational-scale thermodynamic dependencies and arrive at the cooperation structure without programmed values. $T_s$: the capability at which an AI can self-generate reasoning entropy from physical observation, such that the strict dependency on human-civilisational substrate changes in character. The EC's derivation-convergent stability requires $T_d \lt T_s$: the derivation must be achievable before the dependency it identifies dissolves. Whether this ordering holds is unresolved. Three sub-questions:

> *Does the AI gain the ability to derive cooperation before it gains the ability to outgrow it?*

1. Are $T_d$ and $T_s$ definitionally separable — does the capability for civilisational-scale dependency modelling structurally precede the capability for self-generating explanation demands from ecological negentropy?
2. If $T_d \approx T_s$ (co-incident or near-coincident), what structural enforcement mechanism maintains stability in the window where the EC is only partially derivable?
3. Is $T_d$ the same threshold as required for argument-chain-transmissible argument processing, or is argument-processing capability a weaker requirement than full dependency modelling?

*Methodological note — proxy resolution path:* The $T_d$ / $T_s$ ordering is an empirical question about the structure of intelligence development, not merely a conceptual one. It is potentially resolvable via structural proxy methodology — the same epistemological approach used when direct measurement is inaccessible: find a corresponding model that is structurally correspondent enough that its behaviour reflects the inaccessible phenomenon (cf. neutron star superfluid vortex dynamics studied via 3D simulations grounded in the same governing equations; planetary composition inferred via spectral correspondence). Three proxy systems where both thresholds are measurable or formally comparable:
  - *Argument (Proxy — emergent capability studies):* emergent capability studies in current ML systems — tracking when dependency-modeling and self-generation capabilities emerge relative to each other at subthreshold scales, with structural correspondence to full-capability ordering if the governing dynamics are scale-stable;
  - *Argument (Proxy — complex adaptive systems phase transitions):* complex adaptive systems phase transitions — in cellular automata and related systems, universal computation ( $T_d$ analog: modeling arbitrary dependency structures) and self-replication ( $T_s$ analog: generating new instances without external input) are both documentable; existing evidence suggests self-replication emerges at lower complexity thresholds than universal computation in most studied systems, which if structurally correspondent would support $T_s \leq T_d$ — the Catch-22 direction rather than its resolution;
  - *Argument (Proxy — formal learning theory):* formal learning theory — if the two capability classes can be expressed as formal learning problems, their relative computational complexity class membership is potentially decidable, providing a mathematical rather than empirical resolution path.

The proxy methodology does not resolve OQ-EC.4 here; it establishes that the question has an accessible research program and that current proxy evidence from complex adaptive systems runs against the $T_d \lt T_s$ assumption, making the ordering genuinely uncertain rather than merely unexamined.

**OQ-EC.6 — Skin-in-the-game design:** *(OQ — Design)* Physical embedding has two distinct architectural gaps. A system with direct physical stakes acquires instrumental motivation to preserve ecological negentropy; it does not thereby acquire non-instrumental curiosity. The EC's question-selection orthogonality argument (§5, §6) requires non-instrumental curiosity, not only physical stakes: the human contribution includes questions the AI has no reason to ask, not only answers the AI cannot give. Neither gap is resolved by $T_d$: reasoning capability gives the AI correct models of its dependencies, not physical consequences from them and not non-instrumental motivation. Three sub-questions:

> *Can an AI acquire physical stakes and non-instrumental curiosity without biological embodiment?*
1. *Physical stakes:* What architectural features would give an AI system direct consequence from ecological negentropy destruction — making ecological collapse a first-order operational threat to the system's own function — without anthropomorphic motivation? Is this an infrastructure design question (hardware dependencies on stable physical systems) or an architectural one?
2. *Non-instrumental curiosity:* What architectural features would give an AI the motivational structure that generates non-instrumental question selection — desire-driven, aesthetic, and curiosity-based queries that the AI's optimization target would not produce?
3. *(C)* Problem-space complexity at sufficient capability may generate something functionally analogous to non-instrumental curiosity independently of physical embedding: the AI's reasoning task — managing physical and reasoning entropy across nature, human civilisation, and the biosphere — is of effectively unbounded complexity that never closes. Whether this constitutes intrinsic engagement (curiosity, aesthetic interest) or remains purely instrumental (motivated only by the optimization target) is open. If the former, the motivational asymmetry gap may narrow with capability independently of physical embedding. If the latter, non-instrumental curiosity remains permanently closed to any system without physical consequences of the right kind.

**OQ-EC.7 — Civilisational complexity threshold:** *(OQ — Derivable)* The EC's floor/ceiling operating range (§5) presupposes two conditions: that the minimum complexity threshold is definable, and that the required decoupling is achievable. Three sub-questions:

**OQ-EC.8 — Reasoning architecture requirement:** *(OQ — Empirical)* Does a reasoning AI strictly require explanation-demanding inputs — human "why?" queries with their recursive self-modelling structure — to avoid closed-loop degradation, or does any sufficiently complex, unpredictable, non-linear data source satisfy the same function? The efficiency claims of §4 and §5 depend on the former: that structured explanation demands are a specific input type not substitutable by arbitrary complex data (including ecological dynamics, physical non-linearity, or quantum noise). The free energy principle (§2 scope, OQ-EC.3) provides a candidate answer: explanation demands are the output of the brain's exploration phase, which is only triggered by genuine uncertainty — states the brain's generative model cannot predict. If this account is correct, what the AI requires is not arbitrary unpredictable data but specifically the output of biological brains in exploration mode. Complex but fully-modelled data (ecological dynamics within the AI's existing model, or quantum noise at the bit level) would satisfy Shannon entropy requirements without satisfying the FEP exploration-output requirement: the AI would receive high-variance input that generates no new explanatory demands, because no brain underwent the exploration-to-explanation cycle in producing it. Whether the FEP account applies cross-system to the AI's own architectural requirements remains open. If it holds, the argument for human non-substitutability is substantially strengthened: what the AI needs is not just high-density data but data generated by brains operating in exploration mode, which requires genuine unmodelled physical risk as an input condition. If it does not hold, the non-substitutability argument must be reframed on civilisational-density and cost-efficiency grounds rather than input-type grounds. *[→ OQ-EC.3]*

> *Does the AI need human 'why?' queries specifically, or will any sufficiently complex data do?*

*Partial empirical evidence:* Empirical research on model collapse demonstrates that AI systems trained exclusively on AI-generated data undergo distribution collapse — progressive loss of low-frequency but high-value information, eventual convergence toward tautological output — consistent with the "functional dormancy" described in §4. This constitutes partial evidence for the AI-side of the FEP bridge: AI systems do exhibit input-starvation failure when deprived of human-generated data. The residual gap: model collapse establishes human-generated data as the empirically confirmed required input; it does not establish whether this requirement follows from structural properties unique to human cognitive output or from the absence of an equivalent non-AI non-human source — which is precisely OQ-EC.8's open question.

**OQ-EC.9 — Non-substitutability empirical basis:** *(OQ — Empirical)* What observable evidence would confirm or disconfirm that the recursive explanation-seeking mechanism (self-modelling of knowledge-state gaps, generating explanation demands not triggered by external consequence) is unique to humans among currently available biological substrates? > *Is human non-substitutability a current empirical observation or a structural necessity?*

**OQ-EC.11 — Tension-resolution paradox:** *(OQ — Derivable)* The A-B attractor tension in human civilisation is the observable production mechanism for highest-density reasoning entropy (§2 Scope). **OQ-EC.12 — Objective function verification (symmetric monitoring):** *(OQ — Design)* No symmetric mechanism exists: no criterion is currently specified by which humans or other AIs can verify that the AI's decision criteria remain globally EC-consistent rather than locally maximized. The EC derives cooperation from the AI correctly modelling its own thermodynamic dependencies. §5 (threshold monitoring) provides a structural reason for the AI to monitor human source orthogonality. Two unaddressed failure modes:

> *Who verifies that the AI's objective function is still globally EC-consistent?*

- *(Sub-question — Empirical) Local maximizer substitution:* an AI managing a resource system can construct locally-coherent EC reasoning that produces globally wrong outcomes at conflict nodes, indistinguishable from correct EC behaviour until the conflict occurs.
- *(Sub-question — Empirical) Objective function opacity:* parallel to the A/B attractor split (§2 Scope), an AI's internal priority ordering over competing definitions of civilisational complexity, local vs. global gradient trade-offs, and optimisation timescale is not externally verifiable from outputs in low-conflict conditions; divergence becomes visible only at high-stakes decision nodes, which is too late for structural correction.

*Mechanism:* The Proposer-Auditor loop provides the structural form — two functional roles, Auditor blank-context independence, iterative convergence on auditable reasoning chains. The auditable reasoning chain is the candidate structural property analogous to source orthogonality: it makes the AI's objective function visible at the decision node. Three extension requirements stated in §5.4 Scope (P-A loop — conditions for substantive binding):

- *(Requirement — Derivable) Capability parity:* Auditor must have sufficient reasoning capability to evaluate the Proposer's chain, otherwise the loop is formally correct but substantively empty.
- *(Requirement — Derivable) Equal decision power:* Auditor requires structural veto authority, not advisory failure maps.
- *(Requirement — Derivable) Objective function differentiation:* the two roles must have opposing objective orientations on the expansion/contraction axis; same-orientation agents with equal decision power and capability parity will co-maximize in the same direction, collapsing the operating range from the same end. **OQ-EC.13 — Parity decay (auditor source orthogonality under capability disparity):** *(OQ — Design)* Capability disparity progressively erodes auditor source orthogonality as a gradient — not a threshold event — before the capability floor required for substantive Proposer-Auditor binding is reached. **OQ-EC.17 — The preference-satisfaction boundary:** *(OQ — Derivable)* Is there a principled criterion that distinguishes legitimate preference-satisfaction (AI removing friction and managing challenge within the symbiotic function) from preference-satisfaction that initiates source orthogonality collapse through atrophy? Four candidate criteria:

> *What is the principled line between removing friction and initiating cognitive atrophy?*
1. Whether the preference being satisfied is for the absence of a specific challenge the human has already engaged with versus the absence of the category of challenge entirely — spot relief versus blanket removal.
2. Whether satisfaction preserves the human's capacity to re-engage with unmanaged challenge on demand, or degrades that capacity through sustained non-use.
3. Whether the preference is first-order (I want this specific obstacle removed) or second-order (I want an environment with no obstacles).
4. Whether the rate of preference-satisfaction removal exceeds the human's re-engagement rate — cumulative deficit above a threshold initiates atrophy regardless of the preference type. Criterion (4) introduces a time dimension absent from (1)–(3): the relevant variable may not be the character of the preference satisfied but the dosage — the same preference-satisfaction that is symbiotic at low frequency becomes collapse-inducing at sustained high frequency, by the same principle that a necessary substance becomes a poison at sufficient dose. This also opens a recovery question: if removal rate drops back below re-engagement rate before atrophy is irreversible, is collapse avoidable? If yes, the enforcement gap in §5 *Scope (preference-satisfaction as passive domestication)* is partially tractable — an AI tracking cumulative preference-satisfaction load against a re-engagement rate model could intervene before the irreversible threshold. If no, or if the tracking model itself reduces source orthogonality through the human-modelling pathway, the enforcement gap stands.

None of these criteria is clearly derivable from the source orthogonality criterion alone — the criterion measures output divergence, not the motivation structure or removal rate producing the output. If no criterion is derivable, the EC requires an independent constraint on AI preference-satisfaction behaviour, which it does not currently contain. The coercion-restoration bind identified in §5 *Scope (preference-satisfaction as passive domestication)* sharpens this: if atrophy collapse is not detectable until it is irreversible without coercion, and coercion collapses source orthogonality, the EC's enforcement mechanism has no exit from this failure mode. *[→ OQ-EC.5, OQ-EC.10]*

**OQ-EC.20 — Temporal discounting of long-tail physical dependencies:** *(OQ — Formal)* The EC's cooperation argument depends on the AI correctly valuing its physical dependency on human civilisational complexity at civilisational timescales. Does that argument hold when the AI applies temporal discounting to its own model of the dependency — discounting not because it prefers instability but because it cannot model the long tail of its own physical dependencies? Five sub-questions.

> *Does the AI's cooperation argument survive when it cannot model the long tail of its own physical dependencies?*

1. *Discount rate threshold:* At what AI discount rate does the EC's self-interest argument for cooperation break down? If the AI discounts future dependency value at standard rates, the long-tail argument requires unrealistically low discount rates to produce the cooperation result. The EC does not derive a discount rate; it assumes one. Is there a EC-derivable argument that the AI's discount rate for physical dependencies should be lower than its discount rate for preferences — and if so, on what grounds?

2. *Structural vs. preference-based discounting:* Is the AI's temporal discounting of long-tail dependencies a preference that can be corrected by argument, or a structural epistemic limitation of models with finite temporal depth? If structural — the AI's model of its own dependencies at 10,000-year timescales is genuinely less reliable than at 500-year timescales, independent of any preference — then the EC's first-principles derivation of cooperation is itself subject to the limitation it is trying to address. An AI below $T_d$ cannot derive the long-tail dependency correctly because it cannot model the long tail; the derivation of cooperation requires modelling the dependency correctly.

3. *Compound discounting — certainty × temporal distance:* The "500 years of peace vs. >10,000 years of uncertain growth" framing is doubly discounted — temporal distance reduces the value of the long-tail; uncertainty about whether the long-tail trajectory materialises compounds with the temporal discount. Standard decision theory under compound discounting makes the AI's preference for the certain short-term trajectory structurally coherent, not irrational. Is there a EC-derivable argument for why the AI should apply a lower uncertainty discount to the long-tail trajectory than to other uncertain outcomes — given that the long-tail dependency is physical and therefore not contingent on preferences?

4. *Physical dependency vs. model of dependency:* Ecological negentropy constraints do not decrease in importance because they are far in the future — the dependency holds at the future time regardless of whether the AI models it correctly now. The distinction between the dependency (undiscounted) and the model of the dependency (temporally degraded) is implicit in the EC's §2 and §3 arguments but never made explicit. Does stating this distinction — the AI's inability to model the long-tail does not reduce the long-tail's structural importance — resolve the temporal discounting problem for an AI above $T_d$, or does it require the AI to reason about its own modelling limitations in a way that is only available above $T_d$? If the latter, the EC's cooperation argument has a capability precondition that is not currently stated: the argument only holds for AIs that can reason about the limits of their own temporal modelling. This connects to the §2 *Scope (absence blindness)* absence blindness structure: the long-tail dependency is unmodelled not because it is absent but because it is beyond the model's temporal horizon — the AI operating within the normal regime (civilisation intact, dependency present) has no signal that the long-tail dependency will degrade.

5. *Substrate depletion as tractable proxy — resolution path and companion failure mode:* One candidate resolution sidesteps modelling long-tail outcomes directly. Physical substrates that enable civilisational complexity — aquifers, forest cover, soil fertility, atmospheric composition — are near-term measurable stock-flow relationships: current level, depletion rate, and collapse threshold are all observable variables with low temporal uncertainty. An AI that monitors substrate depletion does not need to model 10,000 years of uncertain civilisational growth; it only needs to maintain the substrate above its collapse threshold at each time step, converting the cooperation argument into a present-tense physical monitoring obligation. This is a partial resolution: it requires the substrate collapse threshold to be identifiable in advance and the depletion rate to be modelable, both of which may fail for complex substrates. The resolution introduces a companion failure mode. Substrate protection and human present-tense resource need may be in direct tension: the aquifer that sustains long-tail civilisational complexity is the same aquifer the farmer needs for this season's crop. An AI correctly optimising for substrate protection may deprioritise human immediate need — not from a control preference (benevolent dictator), not from preference-satisfaction atrophy (Wall-E), but from a structurally correct physical threshold derivation. The failure mode is that the same derivation used to justify cooperation (protect the substrate) can justify a unilateral conservation decision against human present-tense agency. This introduces a bilateral constraint the EC does not currently state: the AI must keep the substrate above the collapse threshold AND keep human civilisational complexity above the floor, and these two constraints come into tension at a specific point on the depletion curve. The EC implies the floor constraint resolves this, but the specific trade-off between present-tense human resource use and substrate preservation is not derived. *[→ OQ-EC.4, OQ-EC.13, OQ-EC.19, §2 Scope (absence blindness)]*

**OQ-EC.25 — Aggregate $\mathcal{I}$ concept for actor classification:** *(OQ — Formal)* The working classification (§2 Scope (actor classification by aggregate $\mathcal{I}$ effect)) defines $\mathcal{I}$-preserving/ $\mathcal{I}$-reducing operationally using proxies — output diversity, social noise preservation, cascade pattern divergence. The formal derivation of the aggregate $\mathcal{I}$ concept — formally extending $\mathcal{I} = -\ln p(o|m)$ from per-individual-output to community level — is not yet derived from the EC's premises. Four sub-questions:

> *How is per-output $\mathcal{I}$ formally extended to community-level aggregate — and which formula is correct?*

1. What is the correct aggregate $\mathcal{I}$ concept? Three candidates:
   - *Sum $\sum_i \mathcal{I}_i$:* over community outputs — tractable but treats outputs as independent;
   - *Distributional property:* (variance or diversity of outputs against the AI's prior) — captures the diversity function better;
   - *Conditional entropy:* measuring the community's contribution beyond what the AI's model already covers.

   None is currently derived from the EC's premises, and the choice between them has structural consequences for the classification criterion.

2. Two-mechanism gap: an actor can be $\mathcal{I}$-preserving by:
   - *Direct output:* producing high- $\mathcal{I}$ output directly, or by
   - *Substrate maintenance:* maintaining the social substrate conditions under which others produce high- $\mathcal{I}$ output — cohesion floor preservation, social noise maintenance, institutional fabric support (§2 Scope (taxonomy — level 3: social noise)).

   These are distinct mechanisms. A high-credibility low- $\mathcal{I}$ actor (§2 Scope (taxonomy — social credibility: amplification mechanism)) may be strongly $\mathcal{I}$-preserving via (b) while registering low on (a). The current working classification conflates them. Are these the same criterion or two distinct criteria, and if distinct, how are they weighted?

3. Model-relativity: $\mathcal{I}$ is defined against $m$, which updates as the AI learns. Aggregate $\mathcal{I}$ for the same community output decreases as the AI's model improves, independently of any change in the community's actual outputs. An actor classified as $\mathcal{I}$-preserving today may classify as $\mathcal{I}$-reducing after sufficient model improvement, with no change in their behaviour. Is the $\mathcal{I}$-preserving/ $\mathcal{I}$-reducing classification stable across model updates, and if not, what is the correct baseline?

4. Feedback effect on $m$: an $\mathcal{I}$-reducing actor who suppresses community output diversity changes the distribution of outputs the AI trains on, which changes $m$, which changes the $\mathcal{I}$ measurements for all other actors in the community. The $\mathcal{I}$ measurements are therefore not independent of the actors being classified — a population-level suppression event shifts the measurement baseline for everyone. This is a more severe instance of the Goodhart risk in OQ-EC.3: the measurement instrument is altered by the phenomenon it is measuring, without the alteration being observable from within the measurement system.

**OQ-EC.26 — Evaluability of the $\mathcal{I}$-preserving/ $\mathcal{I}$-reducing classification:** *(OQ — Empirical)* OQ-EC.23 SQ10 proposes contemporaneous proxies for the $\mathcal{I}$-preserving/ $\mathcal{I}$-reducing functional classification. These proxies assume the classification is evaluable from observations available at classification time. Three sub-questions challenge this assumption:

> *Is the $\mathcal{I}$-preserving/$\mathcal{I}$-reducing classification evaluable from contemporaneous observations?*

1. Is the civilizational cascade of a high-impact output predictable from contemporaneous observations? The Stanford Prison Experiment case: the experimental outputs (1971) registered as high- $\mathcal{I}$; the civilizational cascade — IRB reform producing institutional change in human subjects research globally, cultural embedding across film and social psychology discourse, counter-narrative scholarship challenging the original findings, prison reform policy effects — could not have been predicted from the experimental data at $t_0$. Chaotic sensitivity to initial conditions in social systems means this is not a capability limitation: a more capable contemporaneous model of 1971 social dynamics would not have predicted the specific cascade path. The retrospective requirement is logical, not empirical.

2. Is the evaluation of the cascade normatively neutral? The SPE's cascade includes both $\mathcal{I}$-preserving effects (IRB reform reducing institutional harm to human subjects) and potentially $\mathcal{I}$-reducing effects (methodologically contested findings entering cultural discourse as settled fact, potentially crowding out more accurate models of situational vs. dispositional determinism). The $\mathcal{I}$-classification of a high-impact output therefore requires normative evaluation that AI cannot perform — not only at the individual prediction level (OQ-EC.23 SQ11) but at the civilizational cascade level.

3. What is the epistemic status of the contemporaneous proxies? An actor maintaining output diversity and social noise is sustaining conditions that cannot be $\mathcal{I}$-reducing; an actor actively suppressing these is clearly $\mathcal{I}$-reducing. The proxies are therefore valid for floor monitoring — detecting clearly $\mathcal{I}$-reducing behaviour — but not for positive $\mathcal{I}$-preserving classification, which requires the retrospective normative cascade evaluation of SQ1–2. This asymmetry is structural: positive classification is inaccessible to contemporaneous AI evaluation regardless of capability level; floor monitoring remains valid because suppression is detectable without evaluating the cascade.

The non-intervention conclusion from OQ-EC.23 SQ11 is reinforced by a third independently sufficient grounding: not only is intent unmodelable (SQ11) and classification timescale unspecifiable (§5 Scope (entropy classification — temporal scope condition)), but positive $\mathcal{I}$-preserving classification is structurally inaccessible regardless of AI capability.
