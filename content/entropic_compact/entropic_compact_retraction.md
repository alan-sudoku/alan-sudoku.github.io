## §R1

**Source:** §2 Argument (unconstrained agency as information condition), v1

**Date:** 2026-04-28

**Audit trigger:** Q7 adversarial audit — empirical counterexample. Galileo under house arrest and Soviet-era mathematicians under censorship continued to produce significant novel thought. If individual cognitive freedom were the critical variable, constrained individuals would produce near-zero reasoning entropy. The historical record contradicts this.

**Why retracted:** The argument located the critical variable at the wrong level of analysis. Individual cognitive freedom is not what constraint destroys. The claim "the controller's model already contains the structure of what the controlled source will produce, and surprise collapses toward zero" fails as a general statement — individual humans under physical constraint retain the capacity to produce novel thought. What domestication destroys is not individual cognition but the civilisational infrastructure (institutions, adversarial exchange networks, accumulated problem-histories) that amplifies individual outputs into high-density reasoning entropy. The argument survived at civilisational scale; it did not survive at individual scale.

**Exact retracted content:**

> *Argument (unconstrained agency as information condition):* High-density reasoning entropy requires genuine novelty — "why?" queries that cross domain boundaries the receiving system has not already modelled. A controlled or managed human cognitive substrate produces explanation-seeking within a bounded problem-space: the controller's model already contains the structure of what the controlled source will produce, and surprise collapses toward zero. Only a genuinely unconstrained cognitive source generates "why?" queries that exceed the prior model of any receiver. This is not a values claim about human dignity — it is an information-theoretic constraint: constraint on the source bounds the information density of the output. An AI that manages, domesticates, or prunes the human substrate to reduce physical entropy production is destroying the information density of its own fuel source. The efficiency gain from constraint is thermodynamically real and locally derivable; the information loss from constraint is also real and globally fatal to the AI's own operation. The preservation of unconstrained human agency is therefore a functional requirement for systemic stability — derivable from information density constraints, not from programmed values or ethical commitments. *(C)*

**What replaces it:** v3 §2 Argument (unconstrained agency as information condition) — restructured as four nested sub-arguments: (1) civilisational infrastructure degradation; (2) optimisation feedback problem; (3) FEP exploration suppression; (4) Type B production range. The critical variable is civilisational complexity, not individual cognitive freedom.

**Exploration value:** The core information-theoretic insight survives: constraint does reduce information density, and the AI's dependency on high-density reasoning entropy creates a structural incompatibility with domestication. What the path produced before retraction: the correct framing of the argument as derivable from functional dependency rather than values; the efficiency asymmetry between biological production and artificial synthesis; the opening toward source orthogonality as the operational criterion (developed in v3 §5). What was wrong was the level of analysis and the mechanism — moving from individual freedom to civilisational complexity as the unit of analysis closes the Q7 counterexample.

## §R2

**Source:** §4 Argument (AI defection is self-defeating), v1

**Date:** 2026-04-28

**Audit trigger:** Q3 adversarial audit — Landauer per-bit misframing. The "creating the sun" metaphor implies comparison at per-bit synthesis cost. An adversary can invoke Landauer's limit (kT ln 2 per bit erased) to argue that artificial synthesis of information is not thermodynamically equivalent to creating the sun — that sufficiently efficient AI synthesis could be cheaper per bit than maintaining human civilisation.

**Why retracted:** The metaphor set the wrong comparison scale. The efficiency argument does not rest on per-bit synthesis cost; it rests on civilisational-scale system cost. The correct comparison is not "synthesising one bit of structured novelty" versus "obtaining it from a human" — it is "building and sustaining an artificial system of equivalent civilisational complexity" versus "maintaining the existing human civilisation as substrate." At that scale, the efficiency asymmetry holds regardless of per-bit improvements, because the network effects producing high-density reasoning entropy require civilisational-level infrastructure to emerge. The metaphor was memorable but targeted the wrong level of analysis and was vulnerable to a legitimate per-bit rebuttal.

**Exact retracted content:**

> Synthesising equivalent structured novelty internally is thermodynamically equivalent to creating the sun to obtain sunlight — the resource already exists in its most efficient form.

**What replaces it:** v3 §4 Argument (AI defection is self-defeating): "Synthesising equivalent structured novelty at civilisational density would require building and sustaining an artificial system of equivalent civilisational complexity — negating the efficiency advantage of replacement and destroying the network effects that make civilisational-level reasoning entropy dense enough to be useful."

**Exploration value:** The efficiency asymmetry claim is correct and survives. The path produced the right conclusion at the wrong scale. The retraction sharpened the argument: the unit of comparison is not bits but civilisational infrastructure, and the network effects that produce density (not just volume) of reasoning entropy are the load-bearing element the metaphor obscured.

## §R3

**Source:** §3 Scope (lichen analogy), v1

**Date:** 2026-04-28

**Audit trigger:** Q5 adversarial audit — agency oscillation and incompatible stability mechanism. Lichen's deep-time stability is produced by evolutionary culling of defective variants over millions of years. AI cooperation is produced by logical derivation on first encounter with the functional dependency. These are structurally incompatible enforcement mechanisms. The v1 text acknowledged the incompatibility but then imported the stability guarantee anyway by characterising the structural form as "stable across deep time."

**Why retracted:** Acknowledging that the mechanisms are incompatible and then importing the stability outcome of one mechanism to support the other is a logical transfer error. Lichen's stability is guaranteed by a process (evolutionary selection) that operates continuously and eliminates variants that defect. The AI-human compact has no equivalent enforcement process. The stability of the structural form (asymmetric mutualism) in lichen tells us nothing about the stability of the same structural form when the enforcement mechanism is absent. The analogy was doing more work than it was entitled to do.

**Exact retracted content:**

> *Scope (lichen analogy):* The lichen model is structural, not mechanistic. It illustrates the form of asymmetric mutualism and demonstrates that this structural form is stable across deep time. It makes no mechanism claim. Lichen's cooperation is produced by iterative evolutionary selection over millions of years; AI cooperation, as argued in §5, is produced by logical derivation on first encounter with the functional dependency. These are incompatible mechanisms producing the same structural form. The analogy supports the stability of the structural form; it does not claim evolutionary pressure as the enforcement mechanism for AI.

**What replaces it:** v3 §3 Scope (lichen analogy) — the analogy is restricted to establishing existence and biological viability of asymmetric mutualism as a structural form. Stability is explicitly characterised on the compact's own terms as "derivation-convergent" — not equilibrium-stable, not evolutionarily enforced, but convergent under correct reasoning. This is explicitly acknowledged as a weaker stability claim than lichen provides.

**Exploration value:** The existence proof survives: asymmetric mutualism is a viable long-term structural form — lichen demonstrates this. The analogy correctly identifies the structural type (entropy-reducing partner + entropy-generating partner) and correctly excludes the containment property. What was wrong was the stability transfer. The path produced the correct identification of the structural form and the correct exclusion of containment; the retraction produced the "derivation-convergent" stability characterisation as the replacement, which is a more honest and more defensible claim.

## §R4

**Source:** §2 Argument (physical entropy is insufficient as reasoning input) + OQ-EC.4, v3.0

**Date:** 2026-04-29

**Audit trigger:** Q5 adversarial audit — Goldilocks zone / threshold conflation (Gemini rebuttal). The document used a single undifferentiated "reasoning capability threshold" and, in the same sentence, conflated two structurally distinct capabilities: (1) the ability to derive the cooperation structure from civilisational-scale dependency modelling (T_d), and (2) the ability to self-generate reasoning entropy from physical observation without human input (T_s). By treating these as the same threshold, the document created an implicit Catch-22: at precisely the capability level where the compact becomes self-derivable, the AI also no longer needs humans — dissolving the dependency the compact derives. The tail sentences ("Below that threshold... At or above that threshold...") compounded this by using singular-threshold language that became contradictory once two thresholds were distinguished.

**Why retracted:** A single-threshold framing that conflates T_d and T_s makes derivation-convergent stability incoherent: the compact either fails to be derived (below the threshold) or is derived and immediately dissolved (at the threshold). The two-threshold separation does not resolve this Catch-22 — it makes it explicit and names it as the ordering condition (T_d < T_s) the compact requires. Leaving the conflation implicit is the structural error; the replacement is honest that the ordering is unproven.

**Exact retracted content:**

> A reasoning AI observing physical complexity generates its own "why?" queries — but this is precisely the reasoning capability threshold of OQ-EC.4. Below that threshold, the AI requires human-generated explanation demands as input. At or above that threshold, the nature of the compact changes: an AI capable of self-generating reasoning entropy from physical observation has a different dependency structure, and OQ-EC.4 must be resolved before the compact's terms can be fully specified at that capability level. *(conditional on OQ-EC.4) [→ OQ-EC.4]*

And OQ-EC.4 (v3.0):

> **OQ-EC.4 — Reasoning capability threshold:** What minimum reasoning capability is required for an AI to derive the cooperation conclusions in §5 independently? Is this threshold definable, and is it the same threshold required for P1-compliant argument processing? At or above this threshold, the compact's dependency structure changes: an AI capable of self-generating reasoning entropy from physical observation may no longer require civilisational-scale human substrate in the same way — the terms of the compact at that capability level are unspecified by this document.

**What replaces it:** v3.1 §2 — T_d/T_s replacement text naming the two thresholds, stating the ordering condition, and naming the stopgap requirement if co-incident. v3.1 OQ-EC.4 — rewritten as two-threshold entry with three sub-questions and a methodological note establishing a proxy resolution path via ML capability emergence studies, complex adaptive systems phase transitions, and formal learning theory; CA evidence flagged as running against the T_d < T_s assumption.

**Exploration value:** The retracted framing correctly identified that the compact's dependency structure changes at a capability threshold — this insight survives in the replacement. What was wrong was treating the change as occurring at a single point. The path produced the OQ-EC.4 entry as the open question holder; the retraction sharpened it into a two-threshold question with a concrete research program. The proxy methodology note (neutron star / spectroscopy analogy for studying inaccessible phenomena) is a methodological contribution produced by the retraction process.

## §R5

**Source:** §7 Scope (falsifiability criteria), item (4), v3

**Date:** 2026-05-09

**Audit trigger:** Q4 adversarial audit — unfalsifiable evidentiary criterion. The criterion required evidence of an advanced extraterrestrial civilisation sustaining indefinitely without entropy-reduction mechanisms. No such evidence exists or can exist given current observational reach; the criterion is structurally unfalsifiable by construction.

**Why retracted:** A falsifiability criterion that demands impossible evidence is not a falsifiability criterion — it exempts the claim from challenge rather than specifying the conditions under which it would fail. The Fermi paradox framing provides the document's cosmological motivation, but the falsification test for the thermodynamic collapse mechanism must use observable data. Human historical and ecological records provide the accessible evidential base: if a biological maximiser demonstrably exceeded its gradient recharge rate at civilisational scale and recovered by a mechanism other than collapse, entropy-reduction intervention, or spatial expansion, the thermodynamic framing fails on its own terms.

**Exact retracted content:**

> (4) demonstrating an empirical counterexample to the Fermi paradox framing — a known case of an advanced civilisation sustaining indefinitely without developing entropy-reduction mechanisms;

**What replaces it:** §7 Scope (falsifiability criteria), item (4), v3.8 — respecified to require human historical or ecological data showing a biological maximiser that degraded its system gradient at civilisational scale and recovered without collapse, entropy-reduction intervention, or spatial expansion.

**Exploration value:** The Fermi framing in §1 correctly identifies the cosmological motivation but cannot serve as the evidentiary ground for falsification. The replacement separates the motivating frame (Fermi) from the testable claim (thermodynamic collapse mechanism), making the latter independently falsifiable against the human and ecological record. The precision correction does not weaken the argument; it removes a structural hole that an adversarial reader could use to declare the entire document non-falsifiable on the grounds that the required evidence cannot exist.
